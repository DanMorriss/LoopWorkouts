In short

Loop Workouts has no ads and no cookies, and counts visits and measures its speed only anonymously. Your workouts, exercises, history and settings are saved in this browser, on this device. An account is optional: without one, none of it is sent anywhere. If you sign in, your workouts, exercises and history are also kept with your account, on servers in Europe, so they're the same on every device you sign in on, with your profile (your name, if you give one, and the choices you make when you start). Your other settings stay on each device.

Who's responsible

Loop Workouts is run by Dan Morriss, in the UK, who is responsible for your data under UK data protection law (its "controller"), and is "we" in this notice. To get in touch, email dan@loopworkouts.com.

What's saved on your device

  • Your workouts and your own exercises.
  • Your history: when you worked out, for how long, the sets, reps and weights you logged, and any notes you added.
  • Your profile: the name Today greets you by, if you give one, the body the muscle maps show, what you train for, kg or lb, and the equipment you have.
  • Your settings, such as the theme, and whether sound, vibration and keeping the screen on are on.
  • The workout in progress, so a refresh carries on where you were.

They're kept in your browser's storage for this site. Clear this device, on the You tab, deletes them, as does clearing this site's data in your browser, and so can the browser itself if the device runs short of space. To keep a copy, use Export backup on the You tab: the file goes wherever you save it, and we never get a copy.

What leaves your device

  • Loading the app. Loop Workouts is hosted by Vercel. As with any website, Vercel's servers see your IP address, your browser's details and the pages it asks for, and may keep them in logs for a while to run and protect the service. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Counting visits. Vercel Web Analytics counts which pages are visited, to show how many people use Loop Workouts and where they found it. With each page view it gets the page's address (without anything that could identify you, such as a sign-in coming back), the site you came from, your country and city, and your browser, operating system and type of device. It uses no cookies and keeps nothing on your device: a visit is recognised only by a code worked out from the request, which is discarded after 24 hours, and your IP address isn't stored. None of your workouts, history or other data is sent. See Vercel's Web Analytics privacy page.
  • Measuring speed. Vercel Speed Insights measures how quickly each page loads and responds, to find what's slow on real phones. With each measurement it gets the page's address (cleaned as above), your browser, operating system, type of device, network speed and country. It uses no cookies, keeps nothing on your device, and isn't linked to you or your IP address. None of your workouts, history or other data is sent. See Vercel's Speed Insights privacy page.
  • Signing in, if you choose to. Accounts are run by Supabase, on servers in Europe. When you sign in, your email address goes to Supabase, which keeps it with your account, and sees your IP address and browser's details as you sign in. A sign-in token is then kept in this browser to keep you signed in. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Signing in with Google, if you choose to. Google checks it's you, and tells Supabase your email address. Google knows you've signed in to Loop Workouts, and sees what it would on any sign-in of its own. Nothing else is shared with it. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Sign-in emails. The email with your sign-in link and code is sent by Resend, from Europe, so Resend sees your email address and the email, and keeps a record of sending it for a while. See Resend's privacy policy.
  • Syncing, while you're signed in. Your workouts, their order and your weekly plan, your history (with the sets, reps, weights and notes in it), your own exercises, your notes on exercises, which exercises you've hidden, and what you've deleted of these, and your profile, are sent to Supabase, in Europe, and kept with your account, so your other devices can fetch them. Your other settings and the workout in progress aren't sent. Signing out removes your name from the device. We can reach what's kept there, as whoever runs a service like this can, and don't sell it or share it with anyone else.

Nothing else is sent anywhere.

Why it's used

  • Your account, and syncing it, are the service you've asked for, so we use your email address and what's kept with your account to provide it.
  • Notes that may mention your health are kept with your account with your explicit consent, below.
  • Hosting, counting visits and measuring speed are in our legitimate interests: running the site safely, knowing whether people use it, and making it faster. None of them tells us who you are.

Health details

The body you choose in your profile only picks which figure the muscle maps draw. Notes are free text, so they may mention your health, such as an injury or a condition, which UK law protects more closely. On your device, they're saved like any other note. To keep them with your account, we ask for your explicit consent when you sign in: you tick a box agreeing that notes you write, which may mention your health, are kept with your account. We record when you agreed, and the date of this notice, with your account, and the You tab shows them.

You can withdraw your consent at any time: delete the notes that mention your health (deleting them on one device deletes them from your account too), or delete your account. Either way, they're no longer kept with it. Withdrawing doesn't affect what was done with them before.

Who else handles it

Vercel, Supabase and Resend handle your data for us, only to provide Loop Workouts, under contracts that require them to keep it safe. Google handles its own sign-in under its own policy, linked above. All four are American companies: Supabase keeps your account's data, and Resend sends its emails, from Europe, but each company may reach it from the US. Where data goes outside the UK, it's protected as UK law requires, under the UK's data bridge with the US or the UK's standard contract terms.

How long it's kept

  • On your device: until you delete it, as described above.
  • With your account: until you delete it. What you delete on one device is deleted from your account too, and Delete account deletes all of it at once. Supabase's backups of the database may hold a copy for a short while after, until they're replaced.
  • Hosting logs and records of sign-in emails: for as long as Vercel, Supabase and Resend keep them, as their privacy policies say.

Age

You need to be 13 or older to make an account.

Copy AI prompt

Copy AI prompt on the You tab only copies some text to your clipboard. If you paste it into an AI chat, with a plan of your own, that service's privacy policy covers what you share there.

Your choices and rights

Everything on your device is yours to see and change in the app, export with Export backup, and delete with Clear this device (or by clearing this site's data in your browser). If you have an account, the You tab shows its email address, and Delete account there deletes it, with everything kept with it on the server, at once, and then removes it from your device too. Signing out syncs, then removes your workouts, history and exercises from the device (your account keeps them).

You also have the right to ask us for a copy of your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to have it given to you in a form you can take elsewhere, and to object to how it's used. Email us to ask, and we'll answer within a month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator, or to the regulator where you live.

Changes

Before Loop Workouts saves any more of your data anywhere else, this notice will say so, and the date at the top will change.
