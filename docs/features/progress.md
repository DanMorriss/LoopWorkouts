# Progress

Built: last time on each rep step (PRG-1), personal bests on the workout summary (PRG-2), latest and best on the exercises list (PRG-3), progress on the exercise page (PRG-4), the muscles worked this week on the Progress page (PRG-6), the most rounds in a time limit (PRG-7), a bar per muscle group against the sets a week aimed for (PRG-8), a suggested next workout (PRG-9, on Today and the workout summary), a suggested next weight on each rep step (PRG-5), what to step up next time (PRG-10) and the muscles worked (PRG-11) on the workout summary, the time per week on the Progress page (PRG-12), and the exercises done this week, with their new bests, on the Progress page (PRG-13). Builds on [exercises](exercises.md) and the sets in the [history](history.md).

## Stories

- **PRG-1** As a user, mid-workout, I want to see what I did last time on each rep step, so I know what to beat.
- **PRG-2** As a user I want to be told when I've set a personal best, when the workout finishes.
- **PRG-3** As a user I want a list of my exercises, each with its latest and best.
- **PRG-4** As a user I want a page per exercise with a chart over time, its bests and its recent sessions.
- **PRG-5** As a user I want the app to suggest the next weight to try, based on how my last sessions went.
- **PRG-6** As a user I want to see which muscles I've worked this week, on the body map, so I can spot what I'm missing.
- **PRG-7** As a user I want to be told when I've done more rounds than ever in a workout with a time limit, so I know I'm getting fitter.
- **PRG-8** As a user I want a bar per muscle group for this week's sets, so I can see which I'm overworking and which I'm underworking.
- **PRG-9** As a user I want the history to suggest which of my workouts to do next, one that works the muscles I've used least this week.
- **PRG-10** As a user I want the workout summary to tell me which exercises to step up next time, with more weight or more reps, when I got every set's reps, and to put it on the workout with one tap.
- **PRG-11** As a user I want the workout summary to show which muscles the workout worked, on the body map.
- **PRG-12** As a user I want a chart of the time I've spent working out each week, so I can see whether I'm keeping it up.
- **PRG-13** As a user I want to see how many reps (or how long) I've done of each exercise in the last 7 days, and which set a personal best, so I can see what my week's training added up to.

## Rules

### Sessions

- A **session** of an exercise is one workout in the history with sets of it: its sets linked to the exercise, from every step that was it. Sets with no exercise (an unnamed rep step) aren't anyone's.
- Bests count **across all workouts** the exercise is in, not per workout (EXR-1), and only from logged sets, so a stopped workout counts for the sets that were done.

### What it's measured by

- **Weight**, where any set of it had a weight; otherwise **reps**, where it has any rep sets; otherwise (timed sets only) **time**. It's worked out from the sets logged, not from the exercise's "Reps" or "Timed".

### Personal bests (PRG-4, PRG-2)

- **Heaviest**: the heaviest weight lifted for at least 1 rep, with the most reps done at it, e.g. "100 kg × 5".
- **Estimated one-rep max**, by the Epley formula: weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30), to the nearest 0.5 in the unit shown; a single rep is the weight itself. Shown with the set it came from, e.g. "116.5 kg, from 100 kg × 5". It isn't capped at the weight wheel's top.
- **Most reps** in one set, with its weight if it had one, e.g. "12 @ 60 kg", or "25" for bodyweight. Of equal reps, the heavier set.
- **Longest**: the longest timed set, e.g. "1:30". Timed sets are only logged from October 2026 (see [history](history.md)).
- Each shows only when a set counts towards it, so a bodyweight exercise has most reps only, and a timed one the longest only.
- On a tie, the **first time** it was done stands, dated then.

### On the exercises list (PRG-3)

- A card of an exercise with sessions adds **Latest**: its latest session's sets as the history writes them, and the date, e.g. "Latest 3 × 10 @ 60 kg · 18 Oct" (the year is added if it wasn't this year). A session with two steps of it joins them with " + ".
- And **Best**, by what it's measured by: the heaviest weight ("80 kg"), the most reps ("25 reps") or the longest ("1:30").
- Cards of exercises not done yet are unchanged.

### On the exercise page (PRG-4)

Under the body map, a **Progress** section:

- With no sessions: "Nothing logged yet. Do it in a workout and its bests and progress show up here."
- **Bests**, as tiles, each with when it was set.
- **A chart**, titled by what it shows: "Estimated one-rep max" (by weight), "Most reps in a set" (by reps) or "Longest set" (by time). One point per session: the session's best of that. A session with no set that counts (e.g. a bodyweight session of a weighted exercise) has no point.
    - **Range**: 4 weeks, 3 months, Year (365 days), or All (the default). A fixed range runs to today; All runs from the first session to the latest. With nothing in range: "Not done in the last 4 weeks." (and so on).
    - The value axis runs between round numbers (whole reps, halves of a kg or lb, round seconds) and never below 0. Dates at each end of the time axis.
    - **Reading it**: hovering snaps to the nearest workout and shows its value, date and workout. With the keyboard, the chart takes focus, starts on the latest workout, and the left and right arrows (and Home and End) step through them; screen readers hear each, e.g. "105 kg, 21 October 2026, Legs".
    - **Show as a table**, at the right of the chart's title, over the range buttons, puts a table **in place of the chart** (since 2026-11-08; until then it was under the chart and added the table below it) of the same points in range: date, workout and value. The button, now **Show as a chart**, swaps the chart back. Being by the title, it doesn't move as they swap, so focus stays on it.
- **Recent sessions**, newest first: the 5 latest, then "Show all N". Each has its date and workout, linking to it in the history (`/history?run=<id>`), and its sets as the history writes them.
- Weights are in the unit chosen in You → Profile.

### Mid-workout: last time (PRG-1)

- On a rep step linked to an exercise, under its reps and weight: the rep sets of that exercise from the most recent workout that had any, in the history's set format, e.g. "Last time: 10, 10, 8 @ 60 kg". A workout with two steps of it joins them with " + ".
- On a step whose sets vary (BLD-11), just **the same set last time**, e.g. "Last time: 10 reps @ 45 kg" on set 2 (both sides, for a step done each side). Nothing for a set last time didn't have.
- Not the workout being done: it's in the history too once it's been ended (and taken back up) or the page closed, but its sets aren't "last time".
- Timed sets don't count, and nothing is shown the first time, or on an unnamed rep step.

### On the workout summary: personal bests (PRG-2)

- Under the sets, **Personal bests** lists each best above that this workout set, e.g. "Heaviest: 100 kg × 5" and "Most reps: 12 @ 60 kg", across every exercise in it.
- Only a **new** best counts: equalling one doesn't (the first time stands), and nor does doing an exercise for the first time, when everything would be a best.
- Nothing shows when it set none, or when it wasn't logged (ended before its first step was done).

### Most rounds in a time limit (PRG-7)

- A workout with a time limit (BLD-8) is scored by how far it got: full rounds, then steps done of the next, e.g. "7 rounds + 3" beats "7 rounds + 1" and "6 rounds + 5".
- On the summary, under **Personal bests**: "Most rounds: 7 rounds + 3", when it got further than every other run of the **same workout to the same time limit** that ran out. Runs stopped before time was up don't count, nor do other time limits (20:00 isn't compared with 10:00).
- As with the other bests, a tie isn't one, and nor is the first run.

### In the history

- **"This week" means the last 7 days** on Progress and Today (since 2026-11-14): the 7 × 24 hours up to now, as "Last 7 days" counts them, so it's never shorter on a Monday. The muscles (PRG-6, PRG-8), the suggested workout (PRG-9) and the exercises this week (PRG-13) all count it. Only the plan's week (see [planning](planning.md)) and the time per week's bars are Monday to Sunday, and the bars are named by their days, not "This week", so the two never disagree on the page.
- Each set line's name links to its exercise's page, where it's linked to an exercise that's still there (see [history](history.md)).
- **Time per week (PRG-12, since 2026-11-06):** a card under "Last 7 days", titled "Time per week", with a bar for each of the **last 12 weeks**, Monday to Sunday on the device's calendar as the plan's weeks are (see [planning](planning.md)), oldest at the left and this week at the right, however far into it. Someone newer sees only the weeks **from the week of their first workout** (since 2026-11-08; until then a new user saw 12 weeks, nearly all empty): those weeks share the width, their bars wider (up to about twice a 12-week bar). Each bar is the time spent in the workouts started that week, as the history counts it (pauses don't count). A week with no time has no bar. The time axis starts at 0 and runs in round steps to at or over the longest week: in minutes ("30m") under an hour, then hours ("1h", "1.5h"). Under the bars, the first week's Monday ("17 Aug") under the first bar and the latest week's ("9 Nov") at the right; with only one week, just its Monday. Until 2026-11-14 the right said "This week", which disagreed with the last 7 days above it.
    - Over the bars, the **chosen week**: its time, big, e.g. "1:26:30", then the week's days and its workouts: "9–15 Nov · 2 workouts", or "28 Sep – 4 Oct" across two months (until 2026-11-14 the latest two were "This week" and "Last week"). It starts on the latest week, and its bar is lit in the time's colour, the others quieter (at 3:1 or more on the card all the same).
    - A **tap**, or the pointer over the chart, chooses the week under it. With focus on the chart, the **left and right arrow keys** step through the weeks from this week, and **Home** and **End** go to the first and this week. A screen reader hears each as it's chosen: "Week of 19 October: 1 hour, 1 workout", or "Week of 26 October: no time, 0 workouts"; the chart's description says "The last 12 weeks", or "The week of 9 November" with only one. Leaving the chart (the pointer or focus) goes back to the latest week.
    - **Show as a table**, at the right of the card's title, puts a table **in place of the bars** (since 2026-11-08; until then it was under the chart and added the table below it): a row a week shown, oldest first, with the week's Monday, its workouts and its time. The chosen week stays over it. The button, now **Show as a chart**, swaps the bars back. Being by the title, it doesn't move as they swap, so focus stays on it.
    - With nothing in the last 12 weeks: "No workouts in the last 12 weeks." It's on the page only with something in the history, as all but the empty message is.
- **Muscles worked this week (PRG-6):** under "Last 7 days" (and on Today, the map without the bars, since 2026-11-06: see [home-and-navigation](home-and-navigation.md)), the [exercises](exercises.md) body map, shaded by the sets of workouts started in the last 7 days: each set counts 1 for its exercise's main muscles and ½ for supporting ones, and each side of an exercise done each side half that, so a left and a right make one set. A muscle with 1 or more shades as main, under 1 as supporting, and the map's lists say which. Sets with no exercise, or of an exercise since deleted, don't count, nor (since 2026-11-12) stretches, which ease a muscle rather than train it. Not shown with no sets to count.
- **Bars per muscle (PRG-8):** under the body map, a bar for each of the 18 muscle groups, most worked first and A to Z on a tie, with its sets this week counted as above. Each track has its muscle's **aim** shaded as a band, set by **Training for** in You → Profile (see [settings](settings.md)): for **Muscle** (the default), **10 to 20 sets a week**, the usual advice for building muscle, and **4 to 10** for the **small muscles** that are rarely trained on their own (abductors, adductors, forearms, neck, obliques, shins); for **Fitness**, about two thirds of that, **6 to 12**, and **2 to 6** for small muscles. The band's **edges are drawn over the bar**, so the aim still shows once a bar has passed it: each edge is a line just inside the band, on the track while the bar is short of it, and a notch cut in the bar (in the track's colour) where the bar covers it. A bar ending on the low edge leaves it on the track; one ending on the high edge covers it. The lines stand out from the track, and the notches from every bar's colour, at 3:1 or more. Under the aim is **under** (a grey bar), within it **in range** (cyan), past it **over** (pink); the bar ending short of the band or past it says the same without colour, and a screen reader hears e.g. "Glutes: 2 sets, under 10". The tracks run to 25 sets (a quarter past the highest aim), or further when a muscle has had more. A key under them says the bands are the sets a week to aim for when training for muscle (or fitness), chosen in You → Profile, fewer for small muscles, and explains the counting.
- **Exercises this week (PRG-13, since 2026-11-14):** under the muscles, a card titled "Exercises this week": each exercise with a set in the last 7 days, its name linking to its page (PRG-4), whose **‹ Progress** comes back to this row, in view and focused, and at its right what was done: "28 reps · 3 sets", the time held for a timed one ("1:45 · 2 sets"), or both for one done both ways ("40 reps · 1:30 · 5 sets"). Screen readers hear "28 reps in 3 sets", "1 minute 45 seconds in 2 sets".
    - **Each side** (BLD-10): each side's sets are counted apart, and the side that did more stands for both, so a left and a right make one set: "20 reps each side · 2 sets" for 2 × 10 each side, and the same for one ended before its last right.
    - **Volume** (since 2026-11-14): where any set had a weight, the total of weight × reps over those sets, in the unit chosen in You → Profile, to the whole kg or lb: "28 reps · 3 sets · 1,680 kg lifted", heard as "28 reps in 3 sets, 1,680 kg lifted". Sets with no weight add nothing, so a bodyweight exercise has none. For one done each side it's both sides together, the total lifted, though its reps are each side's.
    - **New bests:** under the line, each personal best it set this week, with a **New best** tag: "Heaviest 62.5 kg × 10", "Most reps 12 @ 60 kg" or "Longest 1:30", worded as on the exercise page, in the unit chosen in You → Profile. Screen readers hear "New best, heaviest: 62.5 kg for 10 reps". They count as the workout summary's do (PRG-2): only one that beat an earlier session, so not one from the first time it was ever done, nor an equal, as the first time stands; one beating a session earlier in the same week counts. The estimated one-rep max isn't listed, being a guess rather than something done.
    - **Order:** those with a new best first, then most sets, then A to Z. The first **6** show, then **Show all 14** (or **Show fewer**), as the exercise page's recent sessions do; focus stays on the button.
    - Sets with no exercise, or of an exercise since deleted, aren't listed; stretches are, as time held. With nothing in the last 7 days there's no card.
- **Suggested next workout (PRG-9):** a card on Today, under the week and Due today, with nothing planned this week, or on a day of a planned week with nothing planned (then offered as "Fancy a workout today?" and steering clear of the next workout planned: see [planning](planning.md), since 2026-11-06), with the saved workout whose muscles are furthest short of their aims this week (the bars' aims, PRG-8, so lower for small muscles and for fitness): each muscle's sets this week over the low end of its aim, averaged over the muscles its exercises work, its main muscles counting twice its supporting ones, lowest wins. So 5 forearm sets (aim 4 to 10) count as more worked than 8 chest sets (aim 10 to 20). On a tie, the one done longest ago (never done first), then their order on Workouts. It names up to three of its main muscles, furthest short of their aims first, in words rather than their sets and aims, which were hard to read on a card: "It works your Chest, which you haven't trained in the last 7 days." when none of them has had a set in the last 7 days, otherwise "It works your Quads and Glutes, which you've trained least in the last 7 days." (since 2026-11-08; until then "For Chest (0 of 10 sets), the least worked this week."). Under it, **See your week ›** goes to Progress, whose bars show each muscle's sets against its aim. **Start**, the filled main button, starts it, as a workout's Start on Workouts does. It was on the Progress (then History) page too, under the muscles, until 2026-11-03; it's on Today alone now, where you go to see what to do. Workouts with no step linked to an exercise aren't suggested, nor (since 2026-11-12) those of only stretches, such as the mobility workouts, as they train nothing; and a mobility day is never the next workout to leave muscles fresh for; with none to suggest, there's no card. Shown even in a week with no sets, when it's the workout done longest ago.

### On the workout summary: next time, muscles and the next workout (PRG-9–PRG-11)

- **Next time (PRG-10):** under the sets, for each rep step of this workout where **every set got the step's reps**: with a weight, one step up, by the same rule as PRG-5 (the heaviest sets only, 2.5 kg or 5 lb), e.g. "Bench press: try 82.5 kg, up from 80 kg"; with no weight, **one more rep**, e.g. "Push-ups: try 13 reps, up from 12". A step that fell short, or is at the top of its wheel (300 kg, 100 reps), has none, and with none there's no section. The same exercise in two steps stepping up alike is said once. It follows the sets as they're corrected (RUN-7), and goes by the step's reps as it was when the workout ended. Each has **Apply** on its right, which puts the step-up on that step of the saved workout (and of this run, so Back to workout carries on with it, as turning the face's wheel would). The line stays, with "Applied" in place of the button and focus moved there, even though this run's sets no longer reach the new reps. A line said once for two steps applies to both. A workout no longer in the library has no Apply; a ready-made one has (since 2026-11-08), as it keeps your reps and weights (see [library](library.md)). Left unapplied, nothing on the saved workout changes, and the weight one comes up again mid-workout next time as "Try 82.5 kg" (PRG-5). Answering **Yes, use next time** to "Use these settings next time?" after applying puts the steps back to this run's last sets, as it always does. On a step whose sets vary (BLD-11), each set is judged against its own reps, and the step up is the same for every set, so the shape stays: "Leg press: try 2.5 kg more on each set" (or "try 1 more rep on each set" with no weight), and Apply adds it to every set.
- **Muscles worked (PRG-11):** the [exercises](exercises.md) body map, shaded by this workout's sets: main where any set's exercise has the muscle as main, supporting where it only ever helps, with the map's lists saying which. Sets with no exercise, or of one since deleted, don't count; with none that do, there's no section.
- **Suggested next workout (PRG-9):** the same card as on Today, counting this week's sets with this workout's in, under the note. There's no Start on the summary: it's started from Today or Workouts.

### Mid-workout: suggested next weight (PRG-5)

- By **double progression**, from the same sets as "Last time" (PRG-1): where every set at last time's **heaviest** weight got at least the step's reps, try one step up: **2.5 kg**, or **5 lb**. Lighter sets, e.g. warm-ups, don't count. Otherwise, stay at the same weight.
- A step up shows under "Last time" as a button, e.g. "↑ Try 62.5 kg". Pressing it puts that weight on the step's weight wheel, as turning the wheel would (RUN-6): for this set, the rest of the workout and next time. It's a suggestion only; the wheel can still be turned to anything.
- It shows only while the step is still at last time's weight. Once the weight's been changed (by the button, the wheel, or in setup), it's the user's call, and the button goes.
- Staying at the same weight needs nothing doing, since the wheel already has it, so nothing shows. Nor does anything when last time had no weight, or was at the wheel's top.
- **On a step whose sets vary** (BLD-11), each set of last time is judged against **its own reps**, set by set (both sides), not just the heaviest. Where every set got its reps, and each set with a weight was done at it, the whole step moves up one step: it shows **once, on set 1**, as "↑ Try 2.5 kg more on each set", and pressing it adds that to every set with a weight, here and in the saved workout, so the pyramid keeps its shape. Where any set fell short, or a weight's been changed since, nothing's suggested. There's no suggestion per set, which would change the shape the user built; it agrees with Next time on the summary.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given 100 kg × 5 ten days ago and 80 kg × 10 then 60 kg × 12 two days ago, then the bests are Heaviest "100 kg × 5", Estimated one-rep max "116.5 kg" from 100 kg × 5, and Most reps "12 @ 60 kg"; in lb, Heaviest is "220.5 lb × 5".
- Given workouts of 30:00 on Tuesday and 56:30 on Thursday this week, and 10:00 last Wednesday, then the time per week shows "1:26:30" and "This week · 2 workouts" over 2 bars, as last week's was the first workout; the left arrow says "Last week: 10 minutes, 1 workout", and so does Home. With a workout 20 weeks ago too, there are 12 bars, and Home goes to the week 11 weeks ago.
- Given a first workout last week, when I press Show as a table, then the bars are replaced by a table of two rows, and the button says Show as a chart and keeps focus; pressing it brings the bars back.
- Given a workout on Sunday night and one just after midnight on Monday, then they count in different weeks.
- Given 5 reps at 60, 80 and 90 kg in three workouts, then the chart has three points, and with focus on it the latest is read first ("105 kg"); Home goes to the first ("70 kg") and the right arrow to the next ("93.5 kg").
- Given sessions 40 and 10 days ago, when I pick 4 weeks, then the chart has one point; with none in the last 4 weeks, it says "Not done in the last 4 weeks."
- Given a bodyweight exercise, then the chart is "Most reps in a set"; given only timed sets, "Longest set", with Longest the only best.
- Given 7 sessions, then the 5 latest are listed, each linking to its workout in the history, and "Show all 7" lists them all.
- Given 2 × 5 at 100 kg of back squats today, then its card says "Latest 2 × 5 @ 100 kg" and "Best 100 kg"; cards of exercises not done say neither.
- Given a history entry with back squats and an unnamed rep step, then "Squats" links to /exercises/back-squat and the unnamed step isn't a link.
- Given squats of 10, 10, 8 at 60 kg last time, when I reach a squats rep step, then it says "Last time: 10, 10, 8 @ 60 kg".
- Given squats of 10 at 60 kg last week, when this workout is in the history too (ended and taken back up, or the page refreshed), then a squats rep step still says "Last time: 10 reps @ 60 kg", not this workout's sets.
- Given 80 kg × 5 bench press last time, when I bench 100 kg × 5 and finish, then the summary lists "Personal bests" with "Heaviest: 100 kg × 5".
- Given 80 kg × 5 bench press last time, when I bench 80 kg × 5 again, or bench for the first time, then the summary has no personal bests.
- Given a 20:00 time limit run before to "7 rounds + 1", when I get to "7 rounds + 3", then the summary lists "Most rounds: 7 rounds + 3"; given "7 rounds + 1" again, or a first run, then it doesn't.
- Given two sets of back squats and one of bench press in the last 7 days, and a plank 9 days ago, then the history shows "Muscles worked this week" with 18 bars, starting Glutes (2 sets, under 10), Quads (2 sets, under 10), Adductors (1 set, under 4, as a small muscle).
- Given Training for is Fitness and 8 sets of chest this week, then Chest is in range (6 to 12); given Muscle, it's under 10.
- Given saved workouts Legs (back squats) and Push (bench press), and two sets of back squats this week, then the suggested next workout is Push, "It works your Chest, which you haven't trained in the last 7 days.", See your week goes to Progress, and Start starts it.
- Given saved workouts Grip (dead hang) and Push (bench press), 5 sets for the forearms and 8 for the chest this week, then the suggested next workout is Push: the forearms are in range for a small muscle, the chest under.
- Given 22 sets for the chest this week, then its bar runs past the band, both the band's edges show as notches across it, and it's read as "over 20".
- Given 2 × 10 back squats at 62.5 kg, a 1:00 plank and 2 × 10 dumbbell lunges each side yesterday, and squats of 10 at 60 kg and a 0:45 plank 9 days ago, then Exercises this week lists Back squat "20 reps · 2 sets · 1,250 kg lifted" (new bests Heaviest 62.5 kg × 10 and Most reps 10 @ 62.5 kg), Plank "1:00 · 1 set" (new best Longest 1:00), then Dumbbell lunge "20 reps each side · 2 sets", with no best as it's the first time; Back squat goes to its page.
- Given 10 squats at 60 kg this week and the same 9 days ago, then there's no new best: the first time stands.
- Given 2 × 10 squats at 135 lb this week and pounds chosen, then it says "20 reps · 2 sets · 2,700 lb lifted".
- Given 7 exercises this week, then 6 show with Show all 7, which shows the seventh and becomes Show fewer.
- Given 15 sets for the quads, then the band's low edge is a notch in the bar and its high edge a line on the track past it.
- Given squats of 10, 10, 12 at 60 kg last time, when I reach a squats rep step of 10 reps at 60 kg, then it offers "Try 62.5 kg", and pressing it sets the weight to 62.5 kg and the button goes; in lb, from 132.5 lb it offers "Try 137.5 lb".
- Given squats of 10, 8 at 60 kg last time, or a rep step since changed to 70 kg, then no weight is suggested.
- Given 5 at 40 kg then 10, 10 at 60 kg last time, on a 10-rep step, then it offers "Try 62.5 kg".
- Given last time's sets of 12 @ 40, 10 @ 45 and 8 @ 50 kg all got their reps on a step whose sets vary, then set 1 shows "↑ Try 2.5 kg more on each set"; pressing it makes them 42.5, 47.5 and 52.5 kg, here and in the saved workout. Given set 3 got 7, then nothing's suggested; and nothing shows on set 2.
- Given a bench press rep step of 5 at 80 kg, when I do 5 and finish, then the summary's Next time says "Bench press: try 82.5 kg, up from 80 kg"; when I correct the set to 4, Next time goes; when I press its Apply instead, the saved workout's step is 82.5 kg and the line says Applied, with focus on it.
- Given a push-ups rep step of 12 with no weight, when I do 12 and finish, then the summary says "Push-ups: try 13 reps, up from 12", Apply makes the saved step 13 reps, and Muscles worked shades the chest as main.

## Platform notes

- **Web:** the sums are in `src/app/models/progress.ts` (`sessionsByExercise`, `progressMeasure`, `exerciseBests`, `sessionValue`) and the wording in `src/app/exercises/progress-format.ts`. The page's section is `ExerciseProgressComponent` (`src/app/exercises/exercise-page/exercise-progress/`), an inline SVG chart in the `--accent` colour. `/exercises` now waits for the history to load, as `/exercises/:id` and `/history` do. Last time is `WorkoutFaceSlideComponent.lastTime` (with `describeSets()` from `src/app/history/history-format.ts`), the summary's bests `WorkoutSummaryComponent.personalBests` (most rounds with `isMostRounds()` in `models/progress.ts`), and the muscles this week `MusclesThisWeekComponent` (`src/app/history/muscles-this-week/`, on the Progress page and Today), shown with `BodyMapComponent`, whose code (with its drawings) it loads only once there's something to show, keeping its space till then, and `MuscleBarsComponent` (`src/app/history/muscle-bars/`).
