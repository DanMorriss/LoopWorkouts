# Workouts (the library)

## Stories

- **LIB-1** As a user I want to see the workouts I've made and start one, so I can reuse them.
- **LIB-2** As a user I want to edit or delete a saved workout.
- **LIB-3** As a user I want to find a workout in the list quickly (A to Z since 2026-11-06; until then in an order I dragged them into).
- **LIB-4** As a user I want to see which muscles a workout works, when I last did it and what's in it, so I can choose what to do today without opening Edit.
- **LIB-5** As a user I want to see which muscle groups none of the workouts in my plan work, so I can fill the gaps (on the Plan page, see [planning](planning.md)).
- **LIB-6** As a user I want ready-made workouts to start and plan as they are, and to copy one to change it, so I don't have to make my own to get started.
- **LIB-7** As a user I want a ready-made workout suggested for the muscle groups my plan leaves out, so filling the gaps is one tap.
- **LIB-8** As a user I want to search and filter my workouts, and mark favourites, so I can find the one I want in a long list.

## Rules

- The page, the **Workouts** tab (at `/library`; the Library until 2026-11-03), has a **title**, "Workouts", as Exercises and Progress do, with **New** at its top right (the filled main button, small enough to sit by the title), which makes a new workout. The weekly plan isn't here: it opens from the week on Today (PLN-4, see [planning](planning.md)). It was a **Plan** button beside New until 2026-11-03.
- Arriving from **Save** in Create on a new workout (BLD-15, see [build-a-workout](build-a-workout.md); a workout being edited goes back to its Details instead, since 2026-11-06), the workout just saved is **highlighted**, tinted with a bar down its left as the history marks a workout just done, scrolled to the middle of the screen, with focus on it, and screen readers hear "Saved" and its name.
- **One list** (since 2026-11-06): every workout of yours and every ready-made one (LIB-6, see below), together, **A to Z** by title, ignoring case ("Untitled workout" for one with none); on a tie, yours first. Until 2026-11-06 yours were in an order you dragged them into, with the ready-made ones on a page of their own.
- A ready-made workout has a **Ready-made** badge on its card and its Details; yours have none. It's started, planned and favourited as yours are, and keeps your reps and weights (since 2026-11-08), but its exercises can't be changed and it can't be deleted: **Make a copy** makes one of your own to change.
- **With none of your own yet**: "Start any of these, or make your own with New." over the list.
- **Search and filters** (LIB-8, since 2026-11-06), between the title and the cards:
    - A **search** box, "Name or exercise, e.g. squat": a workout shows if every word typed is in its title or in one of its steps' names or their exercises' names, in any case, so "squat" finds a workout with a Goblet squat in it.
    - **All**, **My own** or **Ready-made**, a switch under it: everything, only yours, or only the ready-made ones.
    - **Favourites**, a chip with a heart, shows only favourites, yours and ready-made.
    - **More filters** opens, under them, the **muscle chips** (the Exercises list's: All muscles, then one per muscle), showing workouts with any of those picked as a main muscle of one of their exercises, and **Only what I have** with **My equipment**, the same switch and pieces as on the Exercises list and ready-made workouts (one setting for all three), hiding any workout needing equipment you haven't picked. A workout with no step linked to an exercise works no muscle that's known, so muscles hide it, and needs nothing, so Only what I have doesn't. The chip says how many of these are on, e.g. "More filters (2)".
    - All of them at once: a workout shows only if it passes every one.
    - While any workout is hidden, "7 of 20 workouts" shows over the cards (or "No workouts match"), with **Clear search and filters**, which takes them all off, Only what I have included, and goes back to All.
    - What's chosen stays while the app's open, so coming back from a workout's details finds the list as it was. Arriving from Save in Create, or Make a copy, with that workout hidden, clears them, so it's in view.
- **Favourites**: the **heart** at the left of a card's foot, level with the play button, "Favourite Legs day", a toggle, filled in the accent colour while it's a favourite. The same heart is at the right of the title in a workout's Details. Yours syncs with the workout; a ready-made one's as a record of its own, `ready-made-favourite` (see [data](data.md)).
- Each card has the ready-made cards' look (since 2026-11-05), and shows:
    - The **title**, or "Untitled workout".
    - **How many**, in a badge at its top right: "3 exercises", counting the different named steps, or "4 steps" with none named.
    - The **main muscles it works**, the first four, then "+2 more", and **what it needs** as tags ("Dumbbells", "Bench", or "No equipment"). Neither shows when no step is linked to an exercise.
    - A **summary of one round**:
        - Its **exercises** when any step is named: the work and rep steps' names in order, each once, e.g. "Squats, Bench press", or "Squats, Bench press + 1 more" with more than two. This replaces the times below, so the card says what's in it.
        - With no step named, "01:05 - 00:20" (timed - rest) for a workout of one timed step and at most one rest step. With no rest it shows "- 00:00".
        - "5 steps · 02:15" otherwise, with the length of one round.
        - "4 steps · 01:30 + reps" when a round has rep steps, since they take as long as they take, or "2 steps · reps" when there are no timed steps at all.
    - The **rounds**: the number, or ∞; or the time limit, e.g. "Squats, Push-ups in 20:00", read as "as many rounds as possible in 20:00".
- **Hovering** with a mouse or trackpad: the card under the pointer stays as it is and the other workout cards dim (since 2026-11-05), so it's clear which one you're on. Not while a card's options are open, and not on a touch screen.
- **Opening**: **a tap on a card** slides in the workout's **details** (since 2026-11-05; until then it started the workout). The whole card is a link, "Details of Legs day", so keyboards reach it and screen readers hear its summary with it.
- **Starting**: the big round **play button** in the middle of the card's foot, "Start Legs day", starts the workout from the beginning and opens the workout page. If a workout is already going, it's ended and logged as stopped first.
- **Swipe to delete** (your own only; a ready-made card resists both ways): a card swiped to the left follows the finger, showing **Delete** behind it in red, which turns solid once letting go would delete it. On letting go, where the swipe would carry it decides: past halfway it carries on off the card and deletes it, with the Undo bar; short of that it springs back. Caught again on its way off, it stops where it is and isn't deleted. A swipe to the right resists. Up and down scroll the page as usual; a swipe never starts the workout or opens its details.
- **Hold**: holding a card still for half a second **lifts** it (a little bigger, with a deeper shadow, and a short vibration where the phone has one); let go without moving, and its options open, as on an iPhone. Moving off it puts it down. Moving before the half second is up is a swipe or a scroll. Until 2026-11-06 a lifted card could be dragged to another place, and its options had Move up and Move down.
- **Options** (⋮ on each card, a hold let go of, or a right-click; one open at a time, and a tap elsewhere closes it; above the ⋮ rather than under it where the tab bar would cover it, as on the last card, since 2026-11-08):
    - **Add to plan** (since 2026-11-10), first, on yours and the ready-made ones: picks the days of the week to plan it on, several at once, with Undo (see [planning](planning.md), PLN-1).
    - **Edit** opens the workout in setup (see [build-a-workout](build-a-workout.md), BLD-6). A ready-made workout has no Edit, and no Delete.
    - **Make a copy** (since 2026-11-06), on yours and the ready-made ones: saves a workout of your own, "Legs day (copy)", beside the one it's a copy of, which stays as it is, and opens the copy in Create to change. It's never a favourite, as the one it's from may be. The Undo bar says "Made a copy of "Legs day"", with Undo taking it out again; leaving Create without saving keeps the copy as it was made.
    - **Delete** removes it from the library at once, and the **Undo bar** says "Deleted "Legs day"" with **Undo**, which puts it back where it was (see [home-and-navigation](home-and-navigation.md#undo)). Its history entries stay, under the title it had.
- However it's deleted, the cards after it **slide** up to close the gap, and Undo slides them back to make room as the card fades back in (at once with reduced motion, the fade staying).
- **Details** (LIB-4), the workout's own page (since 2026-11-05; a dialog over the list until then), at `/library/<its id>`, sliding in over Workouts as an exercise does over Exercises. Opened from a run's card on Progress (since 2026-11-08; see [history](history.md)), it has **‹ Progress** instead of ‹ Workouts, going back to that run:
    - The workout's title, with the **Ready-made** badge and its one-line description for a ready-made one.
    - **Start workout** under them (since 2026-11-08), the page's main button, full width, "Start Legs day" to screen readers: starts it and opens the workout page, as its card's play button does, without going back to the list first.
    - **Add to plan** under it (since 2026-11-10), an outlined button, picking the days to plan it on (see [planning](planning.md), PLN-1); once it's planned, **Change days**, with "On your plan: Mon, Thu" beside it.
    - **Muscles it works**: the body map, shaded for the main and supporting muscles of its steps' exercises, with the muscles listed under it. A muscle that's main in one exercise and supporting in another counts as main. With no step linked to an exercise: "None of its steps is an exercise yet. Pick exercises for them in Edit to see the muscles they work."
    - **History**: "Done once" or "Done 6 times", then its five latest runs, newest first, each its day ("Tue 21 Oct"), rounds and time (as in the history, e.g. "4 rounds · 24:10"), and each a link to that run on the Progress page. With more than five, **All in Progress** goes there. Never done: "Not done yet."
    - **What's in it**, last (since 2026-11-03; until then only Edit showed the steps): written out as a ready-made workout's preview is (see below), how many times it goes round ("Once through", "3 rounds of all of it", "As many rounds as you can in 20:00"), then its headings, each exercise with its sets ("Back squat 3 × 10, rest 1:00"), and each superset with its rounds and steps set in under it. An unnamed step goes by its kind, as in the history: "Work" or "Reps". An exercise linked to one that still exists is a link, with a **›** at the end of its line to show it on a phone, and its whole line pressable: its page slides in from the right at `/library/<workout id>/<its name>`, with the Workouts tab still chosen, and **‹** and the workout's title at its top, or a swipe right, slide it back to these details.
    - **‹ Workouts** at its top, or a swipe right, slides it back off, to the list where it was scrolled, with focus back on the card; **Edit** at the bottom opens it in setup, and **Make a copy** beside it makes a copy (see Options); a ready-made one has only Make a copy. A link to a workout that's gone says "Workout not found".
- **Missing muscle groups** (LIB-5) and **a ready-made workout for them** (LIB-7) are on the Plan page since 2026-11-06, for what the plan leaves out rather than the whole library: see [planning](planning.md). Until then they were under the cards here.

### Ready-made workouts (LIB-6)

- Workouts that come with the app, made of built-in exercises (see [exercises](exercises.md)), in groups by what they need: **No equipment**, **Kettlebell**, **Dumbbells**, **Gym** and **Other kit** (a pull-up bar, dip bars or a step; since the second batch, 2026-11-03).
    - **The first batch** (10): Bodyweight basics, 20-minute AMRAP, Cardio intervals and Core in 15; Kettlebell full body and Kettlebell swing ladder; Dumbbell full body; Gym push, Gym pull and Gym legs.
    - **The second batch** (8, 2026-11-03), for the gaps the first left: **Tabata** (no equipment: four moves, 8 × 20 s hard and 10 s easy) and **Kettlebell AMRAP** (15 minutes), for fitness; **Dumbbell upper** and **Dumbbell lower**, a two-day split at home; **Gym full body**, a first gym workout of machines and dumbbells; **Gym extras**, for the hips, calves, lower back and grip the big lifts leave out; and, in Other kit, **Pull-up bar and dips** and **Step circuit**, for kit no workout used before.
    - Still to come: mobility or stretching, now that its stretches are written, and a band workout, once there are exercises for it (see TODO). They're written into the app, not saved as your data: they don't sync, can't be changed or deleted, and can be improved in any release. Their ids (`builtin:<title-in-kebab-case>`) never change once released.
- **On Workouts**, in the one list with yours (since 2026-11-06), each with the **Ready-made** badge, at `/library/builtin:<its name>` for its Details. Until 2026-11-06 they were on a page of their own (`/ready-made`, which now goes to Workouts), each to be added as a copy first. `/ready-made/<its name>` goes to its Details.
- **Used as it is**: started from its card or Details, planned on any day (see [planning](planning.md)), and a favourite; its runs are in the history under its id, as yours are.
- **Your reps and weights** (since 2026-11-08): a rep step's reps and weight change as on your own workouts and are kept for next time: changed mid-workout (RUN-6), with **Apply** on the summary's Next time (PRG-10), with **Yes, use next time** after correcting its sets, or before running it, on its Details (since 2026-11-10, below). Until 2026-11-08 they lasted for the run only, and Next time had no Apply. They're kept as a record of their own (`ready-made-change`, see [data](data.md)), which syncs, so the workout itself is still the app's: a step that's a different exercise in a later release drops your amounts for it. Its Details then says "With your own reps and weights." and has **Reset reps and weights**, putting it back as it came, with Undo ("Reset the reps and weights of "Gym legs""), which takes focus as the Undo bar does; Undo brings the button back with focus on it, and closing the bar moves focus to Make a copy. Make a copy copies it with your reps and weights.
- **Setting them before running it** (since 2026-11-10): a ready-made workout's Details has **Your reps and weights**, above What's in it: "Set them before you start, for each exercise.", then each rep step that's an exercise, in order, with its sets as the history writes them ("Back squat 12, 10, 8", "3 × 10 @ 60 kg"), the whole row a button ("Change Back squat, 12, 10, 8" to screen readers) with a pencil. Pressing one opens a dialog named after the exercise: "Your reps and weight for each set. They're kept for next time, and change as you go too.", then for each set (or round, in a superset), Set 1, Set 2…, a **Reps** and a **Weight** wheel (in your unit, – for none), and **Save**. One set has no set names. Save keeps them, as the summary's Apply does, with Undo saying what they are now ("Back squat: 12, 10, 8 @ 60 kg"); focus goes to Undo, and back to the exercise's row once the bar's closed or used. ‹ Back, Escape or a tap outside change nothing, focus back on the row. Sets made alike again are kept as one amount; back as the workout came, the record goes, as Reset does. Steps that aren't exercises (rests, a step that's only a name) aren't listed. Your own workouts have Edit instead.
- **Times too** (since 2026-11-11): a timed exercise, e.g. a plank, is listed with its time ("Plank 3 × 0:45", "Side plank 3 × 0:30 each side"), and its dialog has a **Minutes** and a **Seconds** wheel for each set or round: "Your time for each round. It's kept for next time." (a time doesn't change mid-workout). A set at 0:00 says "Each set needs some time." and Save can't be pressed. The heading says what's there: **Your reps and weights**, **Your times** (e.g. Tabata), or **Your reps, weights and times** (e.g. Core in 15). Kept in the same record, with Undo, and put back by Reset reps and weights, as the reps and weights are.
- **Its exercises stay as they are:** a swap (RUN-11) lasts for that run, and the summary has no "use this swap next time" for it. It says instead "Gym legs is ready-made, so its exercises stay as they are. To use your swap next time, make a copy.", or with no swap, "Gym legs is ready-made: its reps and weights can change, but to change its exercises, make a copy.", with **Make a copy**. Until 2026-11-08 it said "Gym legs is a ready-made workout, so it stays as it is. Make a copy to change it, or to keep changes like these for next time."
- **Make a copy**, from its options, its Details or the summary: saves a workout of your own, "Gym legs (copy)", at once, and opens it in Create to change; the Undo bar says "Made a copy of "Gym legs"", with Undo taking it out again. Leaving Create without saving keeps the copy as it was made.
- A copy made with Add to my workouts before 2026-11-06 stays your own workout, as it was, beside the ready-made one.
- What a workout **needs** is every need of its exercises, once; a choice (a dumbbell or a kettlebell) that's met by a piece it needs anyway is left out, as it changes nothing about who can do it.
- From a ready-made plan's card it has its own page, at `/ready-made-plans/<its name>`, only to look at, and back to where the card was: see [planning](planning.md).
- Its steps have **no weights**, as everyone's are their own: Last time and the suggested weight fill in from the first run.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given no workouts of my own, then the ready-made ones are listed A to Z, each with the Ready-made badge, under "Start any of these, or make your own with New.", and New is at the top.
- Given workouts, then the title "Workouts" shows with New at its right, which goes to setup, and no Plan button.
- Given my "Arms" and "zone 2" and the ready-made "Bodyweight basics", then the list goes Arms, Bodyweight basics, …, zone 2; mine have no badge.
- Given a workout with work 1:05 and rest 0:20 for 3 rounds, then its card shows "01:05 - 00:20" and 3.
- Given a workout of five unnamed steps, two of them reps, then its card shows "5 steps · mm:ss + reps".
- Given a workout of Squats, Bench press, Rows and a rest, then its card shows "Squats, Bench press + 1 more".
- Given a workout that runs until stopped, then its card shows ∞.
- Given a workout with a 20:00 time limit, then its card shows "in 20:00" in place of the rounds.
- Given a card, when I tap it, then its details slide in; when I press its play button, then that workout starts and the workout page opens.
- Given a ready-made card, when I press its play button, then it starts as it is, and no copy is made.
- Given I've just pressed Save in Create on a new "Sprints", then Workouts shows Sprints highlighted, in view and focused, and says "Saved Sprints"; given a workout that's since been deleted, then nothing is highlighted.
- Given a card of mine, when I swipe it left past halfway (or flick it), then it's deleted with Undo; when I let go short of halfway, then it springs back. Given a ready-made card, a swipe doesn't delete it.
- Given a card, when I hold it still, then its options open, and it doesn't start.
- Given a ready-made card, then its options have Add to plan and Make a copy, and no Edit or Delete.
- Given Gym legs, when I choose Make a copy, then "Gym legs (copy)" is in my workouts and open in Create, and Undo takes it out again.
- Given my Legs day, when I choose Make a copy in its options or Details, then "Legs day (copy)" is in my workouts beside it, open in Create, and Legs day is as it was.
- Given a workout done twice, when I open its Details, then it says "Done 2 times" and lists both runs, newest first, each linking to it in the history.
- Given a workout of 2 rounds, a heading "Main", 3 × 10 Back squat resting 1:00, and an unnamed 0:30 timed step, when I open its Details, then What's in it comes last, after History, saying "2 rounds of all of it", then Main, "Back squat 3 × 10, rest 1:00" and "Work 0:30".
- Given a workout of Back squat only, when I open its Details, then the body map shades the quads and glutes as main, and the hamstrings, adductors and lower back as supporting.
- Given Legs day opened, when I press Start workout under its title, then Legs day starts from the beginning and the workout page opens.
- Given Gym legs opened, then its Details has the Ready-made badge and its description, reads "Back squat 3 sets: 12, 10, 8, rest 2:30", and has Make a copy where Edit would be.
- Given I've finished Gym legs with every Back squat set done, when I press Apply on "Back squat: try 1 more rep on each set", then next time Gym legs has 13, 11 and 9, no copy is made, and its Details says "With your own reps and weights."; when I press Reset reps and weights, then it's 12, 10, 8 again, and Undo brings mine back.
- Given I corrected a set of Gym legs on the summary, when I press Yes, use next time, then Gym legs starts from those next time.
- Given I swapped an exercise in Gym legs, then the summary doesn't ask to use it next time, and says to make a copy to use the swap.
- Given a copy of Gym legs I added before, then both it and the ready-made Gym legs are listed, the copy with no badge.
- Given My own picked, then only my workouts show; given Ready-made, then only the ready-made ones; Clear search and filters goes back to All.
- Given only a kettlebell picked and Only what I have on, then only the ready-made workouts needing nothing or a kettlebell show, and the count says how many of all the workouts show.
- Given workouts "Legs day" (Back squat) and "At home" (a step "Squats" linked to Goblet squat), when I search "squat", then both show, with the ready-made ones with a squat in them; when I search "goblet", then only At home and the ready-made ones with a goblet squat.
- Given no workout matches the search, then "No workouts match" shows, and Clear search and filters shows them all again.
- Given a card, mine or ready-made, when I press its heart, then it's a favourite (the heart pressed), and with Favourites on only favourites show; pressing the heart doesn't open its details or start it.
- Given a ready-made favourite on one device, when it syncs, then it's a favourite on my other device too.
- Given a favourite, when I edit it in Create and save, then it's still a favourite.
- Given Chest picked under More filters, then only workouts with a chest exercise as a main muscle show.
- Given a search, when I open a workout's details and come back, then the search is still there; when I arrive from Save in Create with the saved workout hidden, then the search and filters are cleared.
- Given I delete a workout, then it's gone at once; when I press Undo, then it's back.

## Platform notes

- **Web:** `WorkoutLibraryComponent` and `WorkoutCardComponent` (`src/app/workout-library/`), at `/library`, and `WorkoutDetailsComponent` (`workout-details/`), at `/library/<its id>` (a ready-made one's id is `builtin:<its name>`). The search and filters are `LibraryFiltersComponent` (`library-filters/`), with what's chosen kept by `LibraryFilters` (`library-filters.service.ts`), and the list cut down by `filterWorkouts()` in `src/app/models/workout-filter.ts` and put A to Z by `aToZ()` in `src/app/models/workout.ts`; the heart is `FavouriteButtonComponent` (`favourite-button/`), using `WorkoutStore.setFavourite()`. `/library?saved=<id>` highlights that workout (the card's `highlighted` input), as Create's Save of a new one goes there, and `/library/<id>?saved=1` says so on its Details, as Save of an edit does, and `/library?details=<id>` puts focus on its card, as coming back from its details does; `/library/<id>?run=<run id>` goes back to that run on Progress instead. The card's look is `styles/_showcase-card.scss` and the parts in `shared/components/showcase/`, shared with the ready-made cards, and what it needs comes from `workoutEquipment()` in `src/app/models/built-in-workouts.ts`. The card's swipe and hold are `CardGestures` (`workout-card/card-gestures.ts`), which uses `project()`, `rubberband()` and `VelocityTracker` from `src/app/shared/gesture.ts`, and springs with `criticallyDampedSpring()` from `src/app/shared/spring.ts`; `canDelete` is false for a ready-made card, which resists both ways. The cards close up and open out around a deleted one with `captureLayout()` (`src/app/shared/layout-change.ts`). Workouts are saved by `WorkoutStore`, which still merges another copy's order (`mergeLibraryOrder()`) though nothing here moves one now. `?ready-made=<its name>` puts focus on a ready-made workout's card, as coming back from its page does.
- **Web, ready-made workouts:** `WorkoutStore` loads `BUILT_IN_WORKOUTS` (`src/app/models/built-in-workouts.ts`) only when asked (`loadReadyMade()`), as they bring the built-in exercises with them: the pages that show, plan or run workouts wait for it (`readyMadeLoaded` in `app.routes.ts`), so pages such as Welcome, Settings and Help download neither. It gives them as `readyMade` and, with yours, `allWorkouts`; `findAnyWorkout()` finds either, where `findWorkoutById()` finds only yours, which can be changed. `isReadyMade()` in `models/workout.ts` tells them apart by id. Their favourites are `ReadyMadeFavourites` (`shared/state/ready-made-favourites.service.ts`), synced as `ready-made-favourite` records. Make a copy is `MakeCopy` (`shared/state/make-copy.service.ts`, with `WorkoutStore.copyReadyMade()` and `copyOfBuiltIn()`), used by the card's options, Details and the summary (`SummaryCopyComponent`). The badge is `ReadyMadeBadgeComponent` (`shared/components/ready-made-badge/`). A run of one keeps only its rep steps' reps and weights: `updateActiveStep()` saves those with `ReadyMadeChanges` (`shared/state/ready-made-changes.service.ts`), and `readyMade` puts them on with `withAmounts()` (`models/ready-made-change.ts`), which leaves out any for a step that's no longer the same exercise; anything else changes only the run. A run of one carries on after a reload on any page, as the store loads them itself when this tab has one going. `/ready-made/:id` redirects to its Details; `ReadyMadeWorkoutComponent` (`ready-made/ready-made-workout/`) is a ready-made plan's workout's page, at `/ready-made-plans/:id`, with `readyMadeCard()` (`ready-made-card.ts`). The workouts are written with the file's small builders (`sets()`, `superset()`, `reps()`, `timed()`, …), and `builtInWorkoutEquipment()` says what one needs. A workout's lines are `WorkoutOutlineComponent` (`src/app/shared/components/workout-outline/`), from `outline()` and `repeatsText()` in `src/app/models/workout-outline.ts`. Each workout is checked by `built-in-workouts.spec.ts`: its exercises exist and are named as they are, it reads back as a saved workout, and its supersets have more than one round.
- **iOS:** a `List`, A to Z, where tapping a row pushes its details and each row has a large play button to start it, with `.swipeActions` for Delete on your own (a full swipe deletes), a `.contextMenu` for Edit and Delete on a hold (Make a copy on a ready-made one), and the same Undo. The search is `.searchable`, with Favourites and the filters as tokens or a filter menu; the heart is a button on the row and in the details' toolbar, and a ready-made row has its badge. Details is a pushed page, as on the web, with Edit or Make a copy at its foot.
- **Open question:** deleting the workout that's running doesn't stop the run on the web; after a reload the run is gone. Decide what a port should do.
