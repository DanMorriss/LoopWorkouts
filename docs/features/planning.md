# Planning

Built (PLN-1–8): a weekly plan of which workouts you do on which days, the same every week, on a Plan page opened from Today; this week's progress on it there; what's due today on Today; and the week at a glance at the top of Today, the way in to the plan; and a tour of the Plan page on its first visit. Kept simple on purpose: no multi-week programmes.

## Stories

- **PLN-1** As a user I want to set which workouts I do on which days of the week.
- **PLN-2** As a user I want Today to show what's due today, ready to start.
- **PLN-3** As a user I want to see whether I kept to the plan this week.
- **PLN-4** As a user I want to see my week at a glance where I see what to do today, and change the plan from there, as the plan is my schedule rather than part of my workouts.
- **PLN-5** As a user planning my week, I want a way to add new workouts when the ones I have aren't enough.
- **PLN-6** As a user I want to start from a ready-made week, so my workouts and my plan are set up in one go.
- **PLN-7** As a new user I want Today to offer me a ready-made week for my goal and equipment, to use in one tap.
- **PLN-8** As a new user I want a short tour of the Plan page the first time I open it, so I know what the statuses mean and find the ready-made plans and the muscles at the bottom.

## Rules

### The plan (PLN-1)

- One plan, of seven days, **Monday to Sunday**, each with any number of workouts (up to 10), yours or ready-made (since 2026-11-06), in the order they were added. A workout can be on several days, but only once on each.
- It's the same every week: nothing moves or carries over.
- **Edit plan** on Today's week (or **Plan your week**, with nothing planned; see PLN-4) opens the **Plan page** at `/plan`. It slides in over Today, as an exercise does over the Exercises list; **‹ Today** or a swipe right goes back. The Today tab stays chosen while it's open. Until 2026-11-03 it was opened by a **Plan** button beside + New on the Library, and went back to it.
- **It's Today's week, made to change** (since 2026-11-25, after a design critique; until then a section per day, each with its Add and ⇄, which looked nothing like the week it edits on Today). Top to bottom: ‹ Today, the title **Plan**, "The workouts on each day, the same every week. Pick a day to change it.", how this week's going, said as Today says it (see below), then the week as Today shows it (the same `WeekDaysComponent`): Monday to Sunday as tabs, each its short name, date, and an icon in a ring of its workouts' plan colours, with a tick once done or an empty ring if one was missed, today picked to start with. Under it, the picked day: its name (today's says "· today"), its workouts, each with a dot in its plan colour, or "Rest day. Nothing planned." with the moon, as Today says it; then **Add a workout**, a dashed row at the foot of the day; then **Swap Thursday with another day**, once anything's planned. Then **Ready-made plans** (PLN-6), and what the plan works (LIB-5). The arrow keys, Home and End go between the days, as on Today.
- **With nothing planned** (since 2026-11-25; until then seven "Nothing planned" days, with a link to the ready-made plans): first **Start with a ready-made week**, "The best fit for your goal and equipment. You can change it after.", the first of the plans for you (PLN-7) as its card, with **Use this plan** as the page's main button, which uses it with Undo, as on Today, focus going to the page's title; then **All ready-made plans**; then "Or add your own workouts day by day:" over the week.
- **Add** opens a dialog, "Plan on Thursday": first **Make a new workout** (PLN-5, since 2026-11-10; until then **Add new workouts**, which went to the Workouts tab and left you there), which slides Create in (`/setup?planOn=3&from=/plan`), with **‹ Plan** at its top; then, under headings, **Your workouts**, A to Z, then **Ready-made**, A to Z (since 2026-11-06), those your equipment can do (with any picked in You) first, under **Ready-made, for your equipment**, then **Ready-made, other equipment** (since 2026-11-25; until then one list, each ready-made one with its badge); each with the muscles it mainly works under its name, e.g. "Chest, Abs and 2 more", to choose by; one already planned that day says "Planned" and can't be picked. Picking a workout adds it to the end of the day, and focus goes back to that day's Add. **‹ Back** at its top, Escape or a tap outside close it, focus back on Add.
- **Made for a day** (PLN-5, since 2026-11-10): Create's **Save** plans the new workout on that day, at its end, and slides back to the Plan page with it pointed out on that day (tinted, with an accent edge, as Workouts points out a new workout), focus on that day's Add, and screen readers hearing "Saved Sprints, planned on Thursday". **Start** plans it on that day too, then starts it. Leaving Create without saving plans nothing.
- **✕** by a workout ("Take Legs off Saturday" to screen readers) takes it off that day straight away, and the Undo bar says "Legs taken off Saturday" with **Undo** (since 2026-11-25), focus going to it; Undo puts it back, focus on its handle, and closed, focus goes to that day's Add.
- **Moving a workout** (since 2026-11-16): each planned workout has a handle (⠿) at its left. **Dragged**, it lifts off the list and goes to another place on its day, or, let go on a day in the week over it, which is outlined while it's over it (since 2026-11-25), to that day, at its end, which is then shown, the others sliding aside to make room on the app's spring, and settles into place where it's let go; a day with nothing on it makes room for it. It can't go to a day it's on already, or a full one. Moved to another day it's planned there now, as Add does (so moved to a day gone by, it's From next week, PLN-3); moved on its own day, it keeps when it was planned. Screen readers hear "Legs moved to Thursday", or "Legs moved to 2 of 3 on Friday", and focus goes to its handle. The Undo bar says "Legs moved to Thursday", with **Undo**, which puts the whole week back as it was, focus going to its handle on the day it came from (since 2026-11-25; until then there was no Undo, and dragging one back off a day gone by left it "From next week"). Focus stays on its handle where it's gone. **Pressed** rather than dragged (by a tap, a keyboard, or a screen reader, as "Move Legs to other days"), it opens the same "Plan Legs on…" dialog as Add to plan (below), its days chosen, with Undo; focus goes back to its handle on the day it was pressed on, or on the first day it's on now. Dragging a workout doesn't swipe the page back.
- **Move up** and **Move down** in a workout's menu (since 2026-11-25) change its place on its day without dragging, for a keyboard or a screen reader, with the same Undo; focus stays on its name. The first workout of a day is the one Today's Start offers.
- **Swapping two days** (since 2026-11-18): **Swap Thursday with another day**, under the day shown, once anything's planned, picks that day: in the accent colour, with "Now pick the day to swap Thursday with." and **Cancel** over the day. The next day picked in the week then swaps everything planned on the two, in one go; a day with nothing on it takes the other's workouts, leaving it empty. Picking the same day again, Cancel, or Escape puts it down. The Undo bar says "Thursday and Saturday swapped", with Undo, focus going to it, then to the second day's Swap; undone, the first's. (Until 2026-11-25 each day had a ⇄ of its own, beside Add.)
- **A workout's menu** (since 2026-11-16): a planned workout's name is a button, with a ⌄ after it (since 2026-11-25) to say so, opening a menu under it: **Start** (as Start on Today's week does), **Details** (its Details, with **‹ Plan** at its top, back here), and, for one not done, not today's and not planned today already, **Do it today instead** (PLN-3, below). Escape, or a tap elsewhere, closes it, focus back on the name.
- Screen readers hear each change: "Push planned on Thursday", and the Undo bar's "Legs taken off Saturday" and "Legs moved to Thursday".
- There are always workouts to plan, as the ready-made ones are there from the start; until 2026-11-06, with none of your own, it said "Make a workout in Workouts first" and had no Add.
- **Add to plan from a workout** (since 2026-11-10): a workout's options (⋮) on Workouts and its Details, yours or ready-made, have **Add to plan** (Details says **Change days** once it's planned, with "On your plan: Mon, Thu" beside it). It opens "Plan Legs on…", "Pick as many days as you like. Each says what's on it already, and the muscles those work.", then the seven days, Monday first, today marked "(today)", each with what else is planned on it ("Push: Chest", or "Nothing planned"), and **Done**. Each day is pressed to choose it or not (a tick, and `aria-pressed`); the days it's on already start chosen, so unchoosing one takes it off. A full day it isn't on says "Full" and can't be chosen. **Done** plans it on the days newly chosen, at their end, and takes it off those unchosen, in one change; the Undo bar says what changed ("Legs planned on Monday and Thursday", "Legs taken off Friday", or "Legs planned on Tuesday, and taken off Friday"), and **Undo** puts the plan back. Focus goes to Undo, and back to Add to plan (or the card's ⋮) once the bar's closed or used; ‹ Back, Escape or a tap outside change nothing, focus back where it was. With nothing changed, there's no Undo.
- A deleted workout drops out of the plan wherever it was.
- The plan is your data: saved on the device, in backups, and synced, as one record of its own (like the workouts' order), so planning on one device and editing a workout on another don't overwrite each other. The plan changed most recently wins.

### This week (PLN-3)

- Each workout on the Plan page says where it is this week, Monday to Sunday by the device's calendar:
    - **✓ Done**: it was run on that day, finished or stopped.
    - **Missed**: its day has gone by without it; its name is dimmed. A missed workout stays missed: it doesn't move to the next day.
    - **Rested**: active rest (since 2026-11-12) whose day went by without it. Active rest is a workout of only stretches, such as the ready-made plans' Mobility flow and Full-body stretch, or one of your own: it trains nothing and is optional, so it's never missed. Still to do, today or to come, it says **Active rest**.
    - **Due**: it's today's, and not run yet today.
    - Nothing: its day is still to come.
    - **From next week**: its day had already gone by this week when it was planned (since 2026-11-04), e.g. Monday's workout planned on a Tuesday, or a ready-made plan used mid-week; its name is dimmed, and it isn't counted this week. One run that day still counts as done.
- Running a workout on a different day from the one it's planned on doesn't count for its planned day.
- **Done on another day, this week only** (since 2026-11-16): a workout's **Do it today instead** (see the menu, PLN-1) has it today this week, at the end of today's, and off its own day, which isn't then missed; next week it's back on its own day. It says "From Tuesday, this week" under its name, and it's Due, then Done, as today's are; Today's week lists it today, "Moved from Tuesday". Today's week offers it too (since 2026-11-20): see The week on Today (PLN-4). The Undo bar says "Legs moved to today", focus to Undo, then its name today. Its menu then has **Put back on Tuesday** in place of Do it today instead, and it isn't dragged (its handle still opens its days); ✕ takes it off Tuesday. It's kept with the plan (`moves`), so it syncs, and a change to the plan keeps it, as long as it's still on that day; a ready-made plan used replaces it with the week.
- Above the week, how it's going, as Today's week says it (since 2026-11-25; until then the Plan page said "1 of 4 done so far this week", counting only the days gone by, beside Today's "1 of 5 done this week"): "**1 of 5 done this week · Next: Legs on Friday**", "All 5 done this week", "Your plan starts next week" or "Only active rest this week" (`weekTally()`).
- **When each was planned** is kept with the plan, by the device's clock: adding one dates it now, taking it off forgets it, and replacing the week (a ready-made plan) keeps the dates of those already on their day and dates the rest now. Undo puts the dates back with the plan. Workouts planned before this came in have no date, and count as they always did.

### The week on Today (PLN-4)

- At the top of **Today**, under the greeting, once there's a workout to plan: a **This week** card.
- With something planned this week: **Edit plan** at its top right (a link with a pencil before it, since 2026-11-19, opening the Plan page), then the seven days, Monday first, side by side, each a tall pill with its short name ("Mon"), its date ("9") and, under them, an icon for what's on it in a ring (since 2026-11-19; until then a row of tiles, each its first letter and a ✓, an empty ring or a dot):
    - **A dumbbell**: training that day. **A stretching figure**: only active rest, which is optional. **A moon**, with no ring: a rest day, nothing planned (or only workouts from next week, PLN-3).
    - **The ring** is split into a segment for each workout that day, in its colour, so a day with two workouts shows two segments, and a day of Full body then a stretch shows its colour and grey. Each training workout has one of the four plan colours (cyan, amber, green and orange, since 2026-11-24, when violet and pink, the reps and work colours, gave way to green and orange), in the order it first comes in the week, going round again past four, so it's the same colour every day it's on (`planColours()`); active rest is grey.
    - **A tick** on the ring: everything planned that day was done. **An empty ring** on it: something planned that day was missed. Active rest gone by without it isn't missed.
    - **Today's** date is underlined in the accent colour (its text stays the strong colour, for contrast on the picked day's tint).
- **The days can be picked** (since 2026-11-19): today is picked when Today opens, and a tap on another day picks it, as do the arrow keys (round the week), Home and End. The day picked is tinted in its first workout's colour (grey for active rest or a rest day). Under the days, below a line, is **what's on that day**: its name and date ("Wednesday 11", with **Today** beside it on today), then each of its workouts, training first, then active rest, each in its colour with its icon:
    - **What to beat** (PRG-17, since 2026-11-24): each training not done says, under its name, its first exercise's top set last time, e.g. "Last time: Kettlebell swing 16 kg × 15", and once done today, a new best it set; see [progress](progress.md).
    - **Today's still to do** each have **Start**; the first training's is the page's main button, the others' are smaller. This was the **Due today** card, under the week, until 2026-11-19 (PLN-2, below).
    - **Any other**, or one done today, says how it went, as on the Plan page: **✓ Done**, **Missed**, **Rested** (active rest gone by without it) or **From next week**; one still to come says nothing.
    - Under its name: **Active rest** (on today beside training, "Active rest, if you fancy it"), or "Moved from Tuesday" for one done today instead this week only (PLN-3).
    - **Do it today** (since 2026-11-20), on another day's workout that could be done today instead, this week only, as the Plan page's menu offers (PLN-3): one not done, not moved already and not planned today too. How it went (e.g. Missed) goes under its name then, leaving it room. Pressed, it moves the workout to today, this week only, and picks today, which lists it with Start, "Moved from Tuesday". The Undo bar says "Legs moved to today", focus to Undo, then to its Start; **Undo** puts it back and picks its day again, focus on its Do it today. Screen readers hear it as "Do it today: Legs".
    - Each opens the workout's Details, which slide in with **‹ Today** at their top, back here.
    - A day with nothing planned says "Rest day. Nothing planned." On today, the suggested workout below offers one anyway (PLN-2, below).
    - The next day's workouts slide in from the side of the day picked; with reduced motion they're just there.
- Under the days: "**2 of 5 done this week · Next: Pull on Thursday**", counting every workout planned this week (but not those from next week, PLN-3, nor active rest), and naming the first training workout on the next day after today with training planned (days of only active rest are passed over) (none on Sunday, or with nothing after today). Once every one is done: "**All 5 done this week**", or "**Done for the week**" with just one. With only active rest this week: "**Only active rest this week**". With everything planned once its day had gone by (a plan started late in the week): "**Your plan starts next week**", and those days show as rest days; screen readers hear e.g. "Monday: Legs, from next week".
- Screen readers hear the days as tabs, "Days of this week", each day in full, e.g. "Monday: Legs, missed", "Wednesday, today: Push, due today", "Tuesday: Mobility flow, active rest, rested", "Saturday: nothing planned", the one picked selected and today the current date; what's on it is the tab's panel, named by its heading, e.g. "Wednesday 11 Today". Until 2026-11-19 the days were a list.
- With nothing planned: "Nothing planned yet. Here are some weeks of ready-made workouts for your goal and equipment, best first, or plan your own and Today shows what's due each day." (with one alone, "Here's a week of ready-made workouts for your goal and equipment, or …"). The ready-made plans your equipment allows follow, the best for your goal first, swiped between with their dots above them, as on Get started (PLN-7; since 2026-11-19, as until then only the first was offered here), with **Use this plan** as the page's main button: one tap plans the week with its workouts, with Undo, and focus moves to Edit plan. The card then shrinks into the week rather than the page jumping (since 2026-11-04): on Apple's no-bounce spring for moving things (a 0.4s response), its old content fading out as the week fades in, with the cards under it gliding up; Get started's Use this plan does the same. With reduced motion it's a short cross-fade. Under it, side by side, **All plans** (the Ready-made plans page, PLN-6; More plans until 2026-11-19) and **Make your own plan** (the Plan page, NAV-6). Until 2026-11-04 it was **Plan your week**, the main button, with "Or use a ready-made plan" as a link beside it.
- With no workouts at all, there's no week: Today's **Get started** card offers workouts to add first (NAV-6, see [home-and-navigation](home-and-navigation.md)).

### Ready-made plans (PLN-6, PLN-7)

- A **ready-made plan** is a week of ready-made workouts (LIB-6, see [library](library.md)), the same every week. Each has two days of active rest (since 2026-11-12, the mobility days) between its training days: **Mobility flow** early in the week and **Full-body stretch** near the end. They're optional (see PLN-2 and PLN-3). Days with nothing on are rest days, at least one a week.
- The first six, each made for building muscle, fitness, or both:

    | ------------------------- | ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
    | 3-day beginner gym        | Muscle  | Gym full body Mon, Wed and Fri                                               | Mobility flow Tue, Full-body stretch Sat |
    | Push, pull, legs          | Muscle  | Gym push Mon, Gym pull Wed, Gym legs Fri, Gym extras Sat                     | Mobility flow Tue, Full-body stretch Sun |
    | Dumbbells at home, 4 days | Muscle  | Dumbbell upper Mon and Thu, Dumbbell lower Tue and Fri                       | Mobility flow Wed, Full-body stretch Sat |
    | Kettlebell, 3 days        | Both    | Kettlebell full body Mon and Fri; Kettlebell swing ladder and Core in 15 Wed | Mobility flow Tue, Full-body stretch Sat |
    | No equipment, 3 days      | Both    | Bodyweight basics Mon, Cardio intervals Wed, Core in 15 and Tabata Fri       | Mobility flow Tue, Full-body stretch Sat |
    | Fitness, 4 days           | Fitness | Tabata Mon, Kettlebell AMRAP Tue, 20-minute AMRAP Thu, Cardio intervals Sat  | Mobility flow Wed, Full-body stretch Sun |

- **The Ready-made plans page** (`/ready-made-plans`), from Today's **All plans** (with nothing planned), Get started's **All plans**, or the Plan page's **Start from a ready-made plan**, under its hint. It slides in over the page it came from, with ‹ Today or ‹ Plan to go back (or a swipe right); the Today tab stays chosen.
    - "A week of ready-made workouts, the same every week. Using one replaces your plan with it, with Undo; its workouts are ready to start as they are." (until 2026-11-06 it said using one added the workouts you hadn't got), then **Only what I have** and **My equipment**, as on the Exercises list and ready-made workouts (one setting), and how many it hides ("5 more need equipment you haven't picked.").
    - The plans, as cards: those made for your goal (Training for) alone first, then those that suit it among others, then the rest, each in the order above. Each card: its title, what it's for, how many days a week it trains, with "Plus 2 days of active rest" under what it's for (since 2026-11-12; under the count until 2026-11-20), the week as a strip of days under their letters, with each training workout numbered and coloured and active rest an empty ring on a dashed day (its days can be moved, see below), those workouts with the days they're on (active rest under "On rest days"), what it needs, **Include active rest days** (since 2026-11-13), and **Use this plan**.
- **Leaving out its active rest** (since 2026-11-13), for those who'd rather rest: every plan's card here has **Include active rest days** (on Today and in Get started too, until 2026-11-24: since then Today's cards ask only one thing, to use the plan, as it comes), "Optional stretching on 2 rest days.", a switch, on as the plan comes. Turned off, the card shows the plan without it: those days are rest days, dashed with nothing on, "Plus 2 days of active rest" and "On rest days" go, and its training is as it was. **Use this plan** then plans the week as the card shows it. Since 2026-11-16 it's remembered on the device, for every card (until then it was only for that card, and on again next time). Active rest can still be added to a day by hand on the Plan page.
- **Moving a plan's days** (since 2026-11-16), to fit your week: on every plan's card here (on Today and in Get started too, until 2026-11-24, when Today's cards lost the strip of days, as the list of its workouts says their days), under "Drag a day to move it, or tap it and then where it goes.", a day of the strip can be **dragged** along the week (on a phone, held a moment first, so a finger going up or down still scrolls the page) or **tapped**, then the day where it goes tapped (outlined in the accent colour while it's picked; tapped again, or Escape, puts it down). Its workouts go to that day and the days between shift along one, as in a list; the letters stay put. The card then shows the plan as moved: its workouts' days, their numbers and colours (in the order they now come), and **Back to its days** under the strip puts it back as it comes (focus to Monday). **Use this plan** plans it as the card shows it. It's only for that card. Screen readers hear each day as a button, "Monday: Gym push", "Thursday: rest", and each move, "Gym push moved to Thursday".
- **Days you can train** (since 2026-11-16): under Only what I have, "Days you can train", "Pick them, and each plan is fitted to your week.", then a chip for each day, Mon to Sun, pressed to pick it (`aria-pressed`), remembered on the device, and **Any day: as they come** once any are picked. With them picked, every plan's card (here, on Today and in Get started) is fitted to them, saying "Fitted to your days: Tue, Thu and Sat.": its training days go, in their order, onto as many of those days as it trains, spread round the week as evenly as they can be (the fewest days in a row, Sunday to Monday counting), and on a tie as near to where they were; each day of active rest goes on the nearest day left to where it was, and the rest days on the others. A plan training on more days than are picked says "Trains 4 days a week: pick 1 more of your days to fit it.", and is as it comes. The card's days can still be moved by hand from there, and Back to its days goes back to them fitted.
- **Seeing a plan's workouts** (since 2026-11-05): on any plan's card, here or on Today, each of its workouts is a link to its page (`/ready-made-plans/gym-legs`), which slides in from the right as other pages do: the ready-made workout's page (LIB-6), its title, what it's for, how long it takes (LIB-9, see [library](library.md)) and what it needs, whether you have a workout called this already, every exercise with its sets, and the muscles it works, but without Add and start and Add to my workouts, as the workouts come with **Use this plan**. **‹ Today** or **‹ Plans**, or a swipe right, slides it back off to where the card was; the Today tab stays chosen. Each exercise in **What's in it** is a link, its whole line pressable: its page slides in at `/ready-made-plans/<workout>/<its name>`, with **‹** and the workout's title at its top, or a swipe right, back to the workout's page, which still goes back to where the card was.
- **Using a plan** **replaces your plan** with its week of ready-made workouts, as they are (since 2026-11-06; until then it copied each into your workouts, using one of yours of the same title instead), dated now, so it syncs as the latest. From the page, Today opens to show the week. The Undo bar says "Your week is planned: Push, pull, legs", and **Undo** puts back the plan it replaced. There's no "Are you sure?", as elsewhere.
- **On Today with no workouts** (PLN-7), Get started offers the first plan for your goal that your equipment allows (see [home-and-navigation](home-and-navigation.md)). No-equipment plans suit both goals, so there's always one.
- Nothing new is saved: using a plan makes an ordinary plan, which syncs and backs up as it always has.

### What your plan works, and Missing muscle groups (LIB-5, LIB-7)

- **What your plan works** (since 2026-11-06), first: "Supporting ones are only worked lightly. Pick a muscle to find exercises for it.", then the body map, shaded for the main and supporting muscles of the workouts in the plan, each counted once however many days it's on, with the muscles listed under it as Main and Supporting, as a workout's Details shows its own. A muscle that's main in one and supporting in another counts as main. Each listed muscle is a link to the Exercises list cut down to it (`/exercises?muscles=triceps&from=plan`), its whole chip pressable. It shows once a planned workout has a step linked to an exercise, as Missing muscle groups does.

At the bottom of the Plan page (since 2026-11-06; under the cards on Workouts until then, for the whole library: see [library](library.md)), once a workout in the plan has a step linked to an exercise:

- The muscle groups that no exercise in a planned workout works, even as a supporting muscle: "None of the workouts in your plan work these. Pick one to find exercises for it." Each workout counts once, however many days it's on; workouts not in the plan don't count.
- Each is a link to the Exercises list cut down to that muscle (`/exercises?muscles=calves&from=plan`).
- **Back from those links** (since 2026-11-06): the Exercises list slides in over the Plan page, with **‹ Plan** at its top (and a swipe right) sliding back to it, scrolled where it was left. An exercise opened from there keeps it: its ‹ Exercises goes back to the list as it was, with ‹ Plan still at its top. Until then the links went to the Exercises tab, with no way back but the tab bar.
- Those only worked as a supporting muscle are the map's Supporting ones, above, so they aren't listed again here (until 2026-11-06, "Only lightly, as a supporting muscle:" listed them).
- None missed: the section is headed "No muscle groups missing" (since 2026-11-25; until then still "Missing muscle groups"), with "Your plan works every muscle group."
- **A ready-made workout for them** (LIB-7): under the list, a card, "Or plan a ready-made workout for them:" ("To work the supporting ones harder, plan a ready-made workout:" when none is missed), with the workout's title, its description, what it adds, e.g. "**Adds Biceps, Forearms, Obliques and 7 more.**" (up to four named; past that, three and how many more), and **Add to a day**.
    - It's the ready-made workout (LIB-6) that does most for the lists: each missed muscle it works as a main muscle counts 3, each it only supports 1 (it's then lightly worked rather than missed), and each lightly worked muscle it works as a main muscle 2. Highest wins; on a tie, the first in the ready-made workouts' order. "Adds" names those muscles, in the app's order.
    - Left out: any planned already, and, with **Only what I have** on, any needing equipment you haven't picked.

## Platform notes

- **Web:** the plan is `WeekPlan` in `src/app/models/plan.ts`, with `thisWeek()` working out where each planned workout is from the history, by the device's calendar (`weekdayOf()`, `startOfWeek()`). It's kept by `PlanStore` (`src/app/shared/state/plan-store.service.ts`) in localStorage as `week-plan`, each change dated by the sync clock. It syncs as the record of kind `plan`, id `week` (`src/app/sync/sync-records.ts`), and is `plan` in backups from version 13. The page is `PlanComponent` (`src/app/plan/`), its days' lists connected CDK drop lists (`cdkDropListGroup`), with dragging in `PlanDrag` (`plan-drag.ts`) and the move itself `PlanStore.move()`, swapping two days in `PlanDaySwap` (`plan-day-swap.ts`) and `PlanStore.swapDays()`, what it shows worked out by `PlanPageView` (`plan-page-view.ts`), and each workout's row, with its menu, `PlanWorkoutComponent` (`plan-workout/`); a move for this week only is `OneOffMove` in `WeekPlan.moves`, made by `PlanStore.moveThisWeek()`, and `thisWeek()` shows it on its new day with `movedFrom`, and Today's card `WeekStripComponent` (`src/app/home/week-strip/`), with its days `WeekDaysComponent` and the picked day's workouts `DayPanelComponent` beside it, what they show worked out by `weekDays()` in `week-view.ts` (a day's mark from `markFor()`, its ring from `ringFor()`) and the workouts' colours by `planColours()` in `plan.ts`. The ready-made plans are `BUILT_IN_PLANS` in `src/app/models/built-in-plans.ts` (with `plansFor()`, `plansForYou()` and `builtInPlanEquipment()`), checked by `built-in-plans.spec.ts`; using one is `ReadyMadePlans.use()` (`src/app/shared/state/ready-made-plans.service.ts`), which returns its undo, with the whole week set by `PlanStore.replace()` and put back by `PlanStore.restore()`; the page is `ReadyMadePlansComponent` (`src/app/plan/ready-made-plans/`), each plan shown by `PlanCardComponent` (`src/app/plan/plan-card/`), shared with Today's Get started and This week through `PlansForYouComponent` (`src/app/home/plans-for-you/`, with its carousel's `dotsFirst`); its week is `PlanWeekComponent` (`plan-week.component.ts`, a horizontal CDK drop list), which the card loads on its own with `lazyComponent()` (`src/app/shared/lazy-component.ts`), so the drag and drop isn't in Today's first load, and the card keeps the days' order (`order`) and gives the plan as moved; a workout's mark is `PlanMarkComponent`, and what the card lists is from `plan-card-view.ts`. Fitting a plan to your days is `fitToDays()` in `src/app/models/fit-plan.ts`; the days are `canTrainOn` in the settings, picked by `CanTrainOnComponent` (`src/app/plan/ready-made-plans/`), and the switch is `includeActiveRest`. Picking a workout uses `WorkoutChoiceDialogComponent` (`src/app/shared/components/workout-choice-dialog/`), shared with an exercise's Add to workout. What your plan works and Missing muscle groups are `PlanMusclesComponent`, the body map `BodyMapComponent` (`shared/components/body-map/`), (`src/app/plan/plan-muscles/`), from `musclesWorked()` and `musclesMissed()` in `src/app/models/workout-muscles.ts`; the ready-made workout for the gaps is picked by `workoutToFillGaps()`, with its line by `listMuscles()`, in `src/app/models/fill-gaps.ts`. The tour of the page (PLN-8) is `TOURS.plan` in `src/app/tour/tour-stops.ts`, started by `PlanComponent` with `TourService.startOnce('plan')`; the parts it lights up have `data-tour` attributes (`plan-today` on today's day only, `plan-workout` on each planned workout, the first winning, `plan-ready-made`, and `plan-works` in `PlanMusclesComponent`), and the title has the id `plan-title`, for focus at the end. The page (since 2026-11-25) is `PlanComponent` with `PlanPageView` (what it shows) and `PlanMoves` (moving workouts, each with Undo), `PlanDrag` and `PlanDaySwap` beside it; its week is Today's `WeekDaysComponent` and `week-view.ts`, moved to `src/app/shared/components/week-days/` for both, with `weekTally()` saying how the week's going on both pages. A workout dropped on a day's tab is found by where the finger is (`elementFromPoint`) as it's dragged, the tab outlined by `dropOn`. With nothing planned, `PlanStartComponent` (`plan/plan-start/`).
- **iOS:** the page maps onto a `List` with a `Section` per day and `.swipeActions` (or an ✕ button) to take a workout off, and `onMove` (or `.draggable` and `.dropDestination` across sections) to move one; Add onto a `.sheet` or `Menu` of your workouts. The week starts on Monday whatever the device's `Calendar` says, as on the web. The week is a row of seven buttons at the top of the Today tab (each ring a few `Circle().trim()` segments), with Edit plan pushing the plan, and the picked day's workouts a section under it.
