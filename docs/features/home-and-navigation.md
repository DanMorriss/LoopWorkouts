# Today and navigation

## Stories

- **NAV-1** As a user I want to get to any page from anywhere, and straight back to a workout in progress.
- **NAV-2** As a user I want the app to open on what to do next and how my week is going.
- **NAV-3** As a new user I want to choose, the first time I open the app, whether to sign in or carry on as a guest, so I know an account's there without having to make one.
- **NAV-4** As a new user I want to say who I am and what I train with once, as I start, so the app is set up for me from the first page, and greets me by name.
- **NAV-5** As a user I want each tab named for what I come to it for (what to do today, my workouts, the exercises, how I'm getting on, and my things), so I find my way without learning where each thing was put.
- **NAV-6** As a new user I want to land on Today once I've set up, and be shown what to do first: add some workouts, then plan my week with them.
- **NAV-7** As a user I want answers to the questions I'm likely to have, in the app, and a way to them from the parts that are harder to follow.
- **NAV-8** As a new user I want a short tour of the app, pointing at each tab and the one thing that matters most on it, so I know where things are without having to go looking.

## Rules

### Where the app opens

- **The welcome screen** (NAV-3, `/welcome`) opens instead of Home on a device new to the app: not signed in, not welcomed yet, and with no workouts or history. That's a first visit, and the first open after signing out, deleting the account or Clear this device, which all empty the device. A device already in use as a guest when it came in (on 2026-10-30) has workouts or history, so goes straight Home, and counts as welcomed from then on.

    - Top to bottom: the ∞ logo, "Welcome to Loop Workouts", "The workout timer that remembers what you lifted. It runs your whole workout, reps and rests included, and next time shows you what to beat.", then signing in as on the You tab (Continue with Google, "or", the email link and its code; see [backup-and-sync](backup-and-sync.md)), introduced by "Loop Workouts is free while in beta. Sign in to keep your workouts and history on every device you use.", then, set apart, **Continue as guest**, described by "Everything stays on this device. You can sign in any time from the You tab."
    - **Continue as guest** goes to Today, and the device isn't asked again (`welcomed` in localStorage; see [data](data.md)). Signing in with the code goes to Today too; Google and email links come back by way of the You tab, which goes on to Today once signed in.
    - There's no tab bar on it, as there isn't on the workout, so it's one choice. Linking straight to another page (e.g. an exercise) opens that page; the welcome screen waits for Today.

- **The setup** (NAV-4, `/welcome/setup`) follows the welcome screen, signed in or as a guest: Today opens it instead until it's finished or skipped (`setup` in localStorage; see [data](data.md)). A device in use before it came in (2026-11-02) isn't asked; its profile is on You.

    - **One question at a time**, each with "Question 2 of 5" and a bar filling up, its heading taking focus as it shows (so it's read out), **Back** and **Next** (**Done** on the last question), and **Skip for now** under them, all fixed to the bottom of the screen, so they're in the same place on every question and it can be tapped through quickly; a long question scrolls under them. Each answer starts at what's saved (the defaults, the first time) and is **saved as it's picked**, so Next all the way through changes nothing, and leaving part way keeps what was answered. There's no moving on by picking, so arrow keys can go through the options.
        1. **What should we call you?** A name box ("Your name"), "Today greets you by it. You can leave it empty." Enter is Next.
        2. **What do you train for?** Muscle ("Get stronger and build muscle: 10 to 20 sets a week on each muscle.") or Fitness ("Stay fit and active: 6 to 12 …"): Training for (PRG-8).
        3. **What equipment do you have?** The equipment chips, as on the Exercises page, No equipment first, "Pick all you can use, or No equipment. …". Next turns **Only what I have** on if any are picked, or No equipment (off if none), so the Exercises list and ready-made workouts show what you can do.
        4. **Which body should the muscle maps show?** Male or Female (SET-5), with the body map under them drawing the one chosen.
        5. **Do you lift in kilograms or pounds?** (SET-4) Starting, the first time, from the device's region: pounds in the US, Liberia and Myanmar, kilograms elsewhere.
    - **Done**, on the last question, goes straight on to **Today** (NAV-6), which says "**You're all set, Sam.** Change any of it on You, under Profile." in the bar at the bottom ("You're all set." with no name), read out as it shows. Nothing is added or started: Today's Get started card offers a workout to add (below). Skipping goes to Today too, saying nothing. Until 2026-11-03 the setup ended on a screen of its own, "You're all set, Sam", offering a ready-made workout with **Start it**, which started a workout before the week was planned.
    - Finishing or skipping **dates the profile** even if nothing changed, so, signed in, the account has it, and no other device asks again.
    - **Signed in**, it first says "Getting your profile…" while the first sync runs: if the account has a profile (set up on another device), it's taken and Today opens, asking nothing. If the sync finishes without one, can't (offline), or takes over 8 seconds, it asks. Once it's asking, it carries on from the questions if reopened, without waiting again.
    - No tab bar, as on the welcome screen.

- The app opens on **Today** (NAV-2), at `/home`; `/` goes there. It was called Home until 2026-11-03, and keeps Home's address, so links and bookmarks still work. It starts with a greeting for the time of day, by name once one's given (NAV-4): "Good morning, Sam" from 5am, "Good afternoon" from noon and "Good evening" from 6pm, for when Today opened. It came back on 2026-10-28, after the app had opened on the Library since the tab bar came in.
- Today opens with **This week**, the week's plan, Monday to Sunday, and the way in to changing it (PLN-4, see [planning](planning.md)), once there's a workout to plan; then **Due today**, the weekly plan's workouts for today, each with Start (PLN-2); then the **suggested next workout** card, with Start, only with nothing planned this week: with a week planned, the week says what's next (since 2026-11-03; before, it showed whenever nothing planned was still due today; see [progress](progress.md)). Then **Muscles worked this week** (since 2026-11-06; on Today until 2026-11-03 too, then only on Progress): the body map shaded for the last 7 days, as on Progress, without the bars, but with "How each muscle's doing against its aim, on Progress ›" linking to them. With no workout done in the last 7 days (no set of an exercise), the map would be blank, so it isn't there (Dan, 2026-11-06). A card with nothing to show isn't there.
- **Getting started** (NAV-6): Today leads a new user through adding workouts, then planning the week with them.
    - **With none of your own and nothing planned** (since 2026-11-06; until then, with no workouts), a **Get started** card takes the week's place, offering a whole week, planned (PLN-7, since 2026-11-03): "Start with a week of workouts, planned for you. Here's one for your goal and equipment:", then a ready-made plan (PLN-6, see [planning](planning.md)) as a card: its title, what it's for, its training days and what it needs, the week day by day ("Mon Bodyweight basics", "Tue Rest", …), and **Use this plan** (the page's main button). It's the first of the ready-made plans for your goal that your equipment allows: to build muscle, 3-day beginner gym, Dumbbells at home, Kettlebell 3 days, or No equipment 3 days; for fitness, Fitness 4 days, Kettlebell 3 days, or No equipment 3 days. Under it, side by side as outlined buttons, **More plans** (the Ready-made plans page) and **Pick your own workouts** (Workouts); until 2026-11-04 these were See all ready-made plans and a link, Or pick your own workouts. Until 2026-11-03 it offered a single ready-made workout to add instead.
    - **Use this plan** adds its workouts (any you have already, by title, are used as they are) and plans the week with them, and the Undo bar says "Your week is planned: No equipment, 3 days", with Undo, which takes them back off. The card goes, the week takes its place, and focus moves to its **Edit plan**.
    - **With workouts and nothing planned**, the week offers the ready-made plan for your goal and equipment, with **Use this plan** as the page's main button, and **More plans** and **Make your own plan** under it (see [planning](planning.md), PLN-4).
    - Before NAV-6 (2026-11-03), Today said "Do a workout, and what to do next shows here." with **Go to your workouts**, whether or not there were any.
- Today has no logo: it's on the welcome screen, and on **You**, under **About** (the loop draws itself in and a light travels round it; it rests where the loops cross with reduced motion on).
- **On the old address** (loop-workouts.vercel.app, before the app moved to loopworkouts.com on 2026-10-28), the app moves itself to loopworkouts.com, on the same page, once nothing would be left behind: each address keeps its own data. That's straight away with nothing saved (no workouts, history, exercises of your own, notes, hidden exercises or plan), or once signed in and everything has synced, as signing in there brings it back. A sign-in coming back is left to finish first. Until then, every page starts with a card, "Loop Workouts has moved to loopworkouts.com", saying what to do:
    - **A guest with things saved:** "Your workouts and history are saved on this address only. Sign in to take them with you: signing in there brings them back.", with **Sign in** (the You tab) and **Open loopworkouts.com**. It moves once they've signed in and synced.
    - **Signed in, with changes not synced** (offline, say): "Some of your changes haven't synced yet: check your connection. Once they have, signing in there brings back everything.", with **Try again**. It moves by itself once a sync goes through.
    - **Added to the Home Screen**, which stays on the address it was added from, so never moves itself: "This Home Screen app stays on the old address, so after that, open loopworkouts.com in your browser and add it to your Home Screen."; or, once nothing would be lost, "Nothing here would be lost now. Open loopworkouts.com in your browser and add it to your Home Screen, then remove this one. If you have an account, sign in there."

### The tab bar

- Along the bottom of every page but the workout's: **Today**, **Workouts**, **Exercises**, **Progress** and **You**, each an icon with its name under it, each wider than 44px on a 320px-wide phone (about 58px). Each is named for what you come to it for:
    - **Today** (`/home`): what to do now. The week's plan, what's due today and the workout to do next. The weekly plan slides in over it.
    - **Workouts** (`/library`): what you can run. Your workouts, New, and the ready-made workouts, which slide in over it.
    - **Exercises** (`/exercises`): how to do each one, and how you're getting on with it.
    - **Progress** (`/history`): whether you're improving. The muscles worked this week, then the log of every workout done.
    - **You** (`/account`): your things. Sync and signing in, your profile, your data, About, and Settings, which slides in over it.
- **How they came to be:** Home was added as the first tab on 2026-10-28, and Exercises moved before Library. Settings was a fifth tab until 2026-10-28; it's now a row on You that slides in over it (see [settings](settings.md)). On 2026-11-03 they were regrouped by what each is for: Home became **Today**, taking the weekly plan from the Library (its Plan button became Today's week); Library became **Workouts** and moved before Exercises; History became **Progress**, the muscles worked this week moving there from Home and the suggested workout leaving it for Today; and Account became **You**. Each page kept its address.
- It's a capsule floating over the bottom of the page, the same shape on a phone and on a wide screen (up to 26rem wide, centred), in a frosted material the page scrolls under. It sits clear of an iPhone's home bar.
- The chosen tab has a highlight behind it, which slides to the next one when you change tab, on a spring with no bounce. A tab is chosen on its own page and the pages under it (an exercise's page is under Exercises; Settings, Help, the Privacy notice and Terms of use under You); the weekly plan is under **Today**; making or editing a workout, and the ready-made workouts, are under **Workouts**.
- Screen readers hear it as the "Main" navigation; the chosen tab is the current page on its own page, and the current item on a page under it (e.g. "Workouts, current" while making a workout).
- Pages leave room at the bottom for the bars, so the last of a page scrolls clear of them.
- **Where a page opens:** a new page opens at its top, whichever tab you came from. Going back by a page's ‹ link (or a swipe right) returns to where that page was scrolled, however many pages deep. Staying on a page, as when a filter changes, doesn't move it, and the browser's own back and forward are left to the browser.
- **A tab left open across an update still opens every page.** A tab running the app from before a new version went live (e.g. one the browser kept in the background) can't find the code for pages it hasn't opened yet, so it loads that page afresh, which brings in the new version. It only tries once in a few seconds, so a page that really is broken doesn't reload over and over. The tour (NAV-8) does the same when its code can't load: the page loads afresh, and the tour can be started again (were it left on with nothing showing, Take the tour would do nothing).

### A workout in progress

- On every page but its own, a workout that's going shows in a **bar above the tabs**, like a music app's mini player: a dot in the step's colour (pink timed, blue rest, violet reps, grey paused), the workout's name, and the step with its time, e.g. "Squats · 00:23", "Push-ups · 20 reps" or "Paused · Squats · 00:23", ticking down.
- **Pause** (or **Resume**) is at its right. The rest of the bar opens the workout.
- **Swiping the bar right ends the workout**, as End workout does. The bar follows the finger, and red **End workout** shows in the gap it leaves, turning solid (with a tick of vibration) once letting go would end it. Let go past halfway, or flicked, it slides off, the workout ends and its summary opens, where **Back to workout** takes it up again; short of that it springs back. Pulled left of where it rests, it moves less than the finger, more stiffly the further it goes. Caught again as it springs back or slides off, it stops where it is and follows the finger from there; caught sliding off, the workout doesn't end unless it's let go past halfway again. Only a finger or pen: a mouse drag does nothing, and up and down still scroll the page. A finished workout's bar doesn't swipe.
- Once the workout has finished, the bar says "Finished: see how it went", with no Pause, and opens the summary.
- The bar rises into place as it appears.
- **The workout grows out of the bar** when it's pressed: the bar's frosted panel grows to fill the screen with the workout fading in on it, on a spring with no bounce, while the tab bar slides down out of the way. **⌄ folds it back** the same way: the panel shrinks into the bar and the tab bar comes back up. Every other change of page is instant, as switching tabs is. With reduced motion, the pages cross-fade quickly instead. A tap mid-way doesn't wait for it: it ends the move at once and lands on what it was over (as it does mid-slide on an exercise's page, Settings, or the theme's fade).
- **Focus comes back to what opened a page** when it slides back out (by ‹, a swipe right, or saving in Create) or the browser's Back returns to it: the exercise's card on the Exercises list, Settings on You, a workout's card on Workouts, and so on, so a keyboard or screen reader carries on from there rather than from the top. It's found again by its link's address, else its id or label, and its place among the page's elements like it (`nav/focus-spot.ts`); something no longer there (a menu that's closed) leaves focus at the top, as before. The ring shows only after keyboard use, as the browser decides.
- The **workout page** has no tab bar. A **⌄** button at its top left goes back to the page you came from (with its address, e.g. a muscle filter), named for its tab, e.g. "Back to Progress". The workout keeps running, in the bar. Opened straight from an address, it goes back to Workouts.
- On the workout page on a phone, the workout's title takes the place of "Loop Workouts" in the header. An untitled workout keeps "Loop Workouts".

### Help (NAV-7)

- **Help** is a row on **You**, straight under Settings and sharing its border: a ? icon, "Help", the hint "Questions and answers, from planning to sound", and a ›. It slides in over You, as Settings does, with ‹ You (or a swipe right); You stays chosen in the tab bar. Its address is `/help`.
- Top to bottom: the title **Help**, a **Search help** box ("e.g. sound, plan, backup"), then the questions in sections, most needed first: **Your data**, **Sound and the screen**, **Planning your week**, **Doing a workout**, **Making and finding workouts**, **Progress**, **Signing in**, and **Where is…?** (a line on each tab, the plan and Settings, for someone new to the app). Last: "Didn't find your answer? Email dan@loopworkouts.com, and we'll help.", the address a link.
- Each question opens to its answer (a disclosure: tap, Enter or Space), a few short sentences using the app's own words for its buttons and pages, with a link to do it where there's a page for it, e.g. **Go to You**.
- **Searching** shows only the questions whose question or answer has every word typed, in any order, ignoring case, opened to read at once, and screen readers hear how many ("3 answers"). With none: 'Nothing matches "…". Try other words, or email us below.' With anything typed, an **×** at the end of the box (Clear search) empties it, showing every question again, focus staying in the box.
- **? links**: a small ? (44px to tap) beside the parts that are harder to follow opens Help on the question about it, opened, scrolled to and focused, so it's read out: screen readers hear the link as "Help:" and the question, e.g. "Help: Why is there no sound?". They're beside **Tap to turn on sound** on the workout page (Why is there no sound?), **Your plan starts next week** on Today, and the guest choice on the welcome screen (Will I lose my workouts?).
- The answers are kept true to the app: a change to what one explains changes it in the same PR.

### The tour (NAV-8)

Since 2026-11-06, from an interview with Dan.

- **Offered once on Today**: a card at its top, under the greeting, "**New here? Take a 1-minute tour**", with **Take the tour** (the main button) and **Not now**. It shows on every device until one of them is pressed, or the tour is started from Help: so straight after the setup on a new device, and once on a device already in use when the tour came in. It's kept on the device only, not synced, and Clear this device (or signing out) empties it, so a device set up again is offered it again. Not now takes the card away, and focus goes to the greeting.
- **Tour of the app** is also in **Help**, in **Tours** under the search box, for any time, beside **Making a workout**, which opens Create on a new workout and takes its tour (BLD-17, see [build-a-workout](build-a-workout.md)), **Running a workout**, which takes the workout page's, on the workout going or a practice run (RUN-13, see [run-a-workout](run-a-workout.md)), and **Planning your week**, which opens the Plan page and takes its tour (PLN-8, see [planning](planning.md)). Tours shows whatever's searched for, as it isn't an answer.
- The tour goes through **seven stops**, opening each page in turn (as its tab would) and **lighting up** one part of it, and **the page's tab** in the tab bar, so it shows the way there; the rest of the screen is dimmed. A card just above the tab bar, which stays in view, says what it is:
    1. **Today**: the Getting started card, "Start here: add a few workouts, then plan your week. Once you have, Today shows what's due and how your week's going." With workouts or a plan there's no Getting started card, so it lights up the week instead: "Your week: what's planned and what's done. Edit plan changes it."
    2. **Workouts**: the first workout card, all of it, "Tap a card for its details, or ▶ to start it."
    3. **Your own workouts**, on Workouts still: **+ New**, "Tap + New to make your own: its exercises, sets, reps, rests and rounds."
    4. **Exercises**: the first exercise card, "Tap an exercise to see how it's done, a 3D demo and the muscles it works."
    5. **Progress**: with nothing done, the page shows **example workouts** (about three a week for 12 weeks, the last three in the last 7 days, of pushes, pulls and legs), with "Examples, to show what's here. Yours will replace them." over them, and the Time per week card is lit up: "Once you've done a few workouts, this shows your time each week, the muscles you've worked and every workout. These are examples." They're only shown, never saved or synced, only on this stop, and never beside workouts of your own; they go as the tour moves on or ends. With workouts done, it lights up their Time per week card: "How you're getting on: your time each week, the muscles you've worked, and every workout you've done."
    6. **You**: the Sync section, "Sign in to keep everything safe and on all your devices. Settings and Help are here too."
    7. **That's it**, back on Today, with the **whole tab bar** lit up: "The tabs take you to each part, wherever you are. Start with a workout, or plan your week."
- Each stop's card has its title (the page's name, or what the stop's about), the line, "**2 of 7**", and **Back** (from the second stop), **Skip tour** and **Next**, which is **Done** on the last. **Escape** skips too. The dimmed page can't be pressed while the tour is on, so it can't be left half-way by a tap.
- The part lit up is **scrolled into view** first, near the top of the screen, so the card doesn't cover it; if a part can't be found (e.g. a page still loading after a couple of seconds), the stop shows its card with nothing lit up.
- **Focus** goes to each stop's title as it shows, so a screen reader reads the title and the line, and stays within the card. **Done** leaves you on Today with focus on the greeting; **Skip tour** leaves you on the page you were on, with focus on its title.
- The lights **move** from one part, and one tab, to the next; with reduced motion they jump. A tab's light shows straight away, a part's once it's found.

### Undo

- Deleting goes ahead **at once**, with no "Are you sure?" (the one exception is Clear this device, which can't be undone; see [backup-and-sync](backup-and-sync.md#clear-this-device-syn-9)): a workout in Workouts, a step in setup (one with anything in it), your own exercise (or hiding one that's in your history instead), a workout in the history. Then the **Undo bar** offers to put it back.
- It floats above the tabs (and the workout's bar), in the same frosted capsule: what happened, e.g. "Deleted "Legs day".", **Undo**, and a close button. It rises in, and sinks out as it goes.
- **Undo** puts it back as it was, where it was, and focus goes to it (e.g. the workout's ⋮ button). An exercise deleted from its page opens that page again.
- It **doesn't go on a timer**, so there's always time to read it and reach it. It goes when it's used, closed (or Escape), replaced by another delete, or the page changes.
- Focus goes to Undo as it appears, so it's one key away, and screen readers hear what happened. Closing it puts focus where it would have gone after the delete.
- **Notices** use the same bar, with no Undo: "Signed in as me@example.com." on Today after signing in, and what happened after signing out, deleting the account or being signed out (see [backup-and-sync](backup-and-sync.md)). They're read out as they show, don't take focus, and go as an offer does. A notice can have a link in place of Undo, e.g. **View workouts** after Import backup; and one already read out on its page (the import's result, said under its buttons) is shown but not read out again.

## Acceptance criteria

- Given a device new to the app, when I open it, then the welcome screen opens, with no tab bar; when I press Continue as guest, then Today opens, and opening the app again opens Today.
- Given a device with workouts from before the welcome screen and no account, when I open it, then Today opens.
- Given I'm signed out after signing out, when I open Today, then the welcome screen opens.
- Given a device new to the app, when I continue as a guest, then the setup opens, on "What should we call you?"; when I type Sam, pick Fitness and Kettlebell and go on to the end, and press Done, then Today opens, saying "You're all set, Sam." in the bar, "Good morning, Sam" (in the morning) at the top, and offering Kettlebell full body to add; opening the app again opens Today.
- Given the setup is open, when I press Skip for now, then Today opens, and the setup isn't asked again.
- Given the setup is open, when I go from question to question, then Next and Skip for now stay in the same place on screen.
- Given a page scrolled down, when I change tab, then the new page opens at its top.
- Given the Exercises list scrolled down, when I open an exercise and press ‹ Exercises, then the list is back where it was scrolled.
- Given the Exercises list, when I open an exercise from its card and go back (by ‹ Exercises, a swipe right or the browser's Back), then focus is on that card again, so the keyboard or screen reader carries on from it.
- Given I set up on my phone, signed in, when I sign in on a new laptop, then it says "Getting your profile…", then Today opens, with my name, goal, equipment, body and units.
- Given a device in use before the setup came in, when I open the app, then Today opens.
- Given nothing saved on loop-workouts.vercel.app, when I open a page there, then the same page opens on loopworkouts.com.
- Given workouts saved on loop-workouts.vercel.app as a guest, when I open it, then it stays, with the card saying it's moved; when I sign in and it's synced, then it moves to loopworkouts.com.
- Given I open the app, then it's on Today, with Today chosen in the tab bar, and the tabs read Today, Workouts, Exercises, Progress and You.
- Given no workouts yet, as a guest with no equipment, when I open Today, then it shows Get started with No equipment, 3 days, no week and no logo; when I press Use this plan, then the week is planned with the ready-made Bodyweight basics, Cardio intervals, Core in 15 and Tabata as they are (Mon, Wed and Fri), none copied into my workouts, the Undo bar says "Your week is planned: No equipment, 3 days", and focus is on Edit plan; when I press Undo, then the plan's gone and Get started is back.
- Given workouts and nothing planned, when I open Today, then the week says "Nothing planned yet." with a ready-made week's Use this plan as the main button, and Make your own plan under it.
- Given a workout done this week and a saved workout to suggest, when I open Today, then it shows the week, then the suggested next workout, then the muscles worked this week on the body map with a link to the bars on Progress.
- Given no workout done in the last 7 days, when I open Today, then there's no muscles worked this week.
- Given the Progress tab, when I press You, then You opens and the highlight slides to it.
- Given Settings, opened from You, then You is chosen in the tab bar.
- Given the weekly plan, opened from Today, then Today is chosen in the tab bar.
- Given You, when I press Help, then Help slides in with every section closed, and You stays chosen; when I search "iphone", then only the three questions mentioning it show, opened, and screen readers hear "3 answers".
- Given the workout page asking to Tap to turn on sound, when I press the ? beside it, then Help opens on "Why is there no sound?", opened and focused.
- Given I'm making a workout, or browsing the ready-made workouts, then Workouts is chosen.
- Given a workout going, when I press ⌄ on its page, then I'm back where I came from, with the bar above the tabs showing the step and its time; pressing the bar's Pause pauses it, and pressing the bar opens the workout again.
- Given a workout finishes while I'm on Progress, then the bar says "Finished: see how it went" and opens the summary.
- Given a workout going, when I swipe its bar right past halfway and let go, then the workout ends and its summary opens; when I let go short of halfway, then the bar springs back and the workout carries on.
- Given the workout page, then there's no tab bar.
- Given a tab open since before a new version went live, when I press Progress (or You, or New), then that page opens, on the new version.
- Given a workout going, when I press its bar, then the workout grows out of the bar to fill the screen; when I press ⌄, then it shrinks back into the bar and the tabs slide back up.

- Given the Undo bar showing, when I wait, then it stays; when I go to another page, then it goes.

- Given a device just set up, when Today opens, then it offers "New here? Take a 1-minute tour"; when I press Not now, then the card goes, focus is on the greeting, and it isn't offered again after a reload.
- Given the tour is offered, when I press Take the tour, then Today's Getting started card and the Today tab are lit up with "1 of 7"; Next opens Workouts with the first card and the Workouts tab lit up, "2 of 7"; Next again lights up + New, "Your own workouts"; Back returns to the card.
- Given the tour is on Exercises, when I press Escape, then the tour ends, Exercises stays open with focus on its title, and Today no longer offers the tour.
- Given the tour's last stop, then the whole tab bar is lit up; when I press Done, then Today is open with focus on the greeting.
- Given workouts done, when the tour reaches Progress, then it lights up Time per week. Given none, then Progress shows example workouts, saying so, and lights up their Time per week; when the tour moves on, Progress is empty again and nothing was added to the history.
- Given Help, when I press Tour of the app, then the tour starts on Today; when I press Making a workout, then Create opens on a new workout with its tour, even if it's been seen.

## Platform notes

- **Web:** `TabBarComponent` (`src/app/nav/tab-bar/`, tabs in `tabs.ts`), the ‹ back link at the top of a page that slides in over another in `BackLinkComponent` (`src/app/nav/back-link/`), new pages opening at their top in `openNewPagesAtTop` (`src/app/nav/open-at-top.ts`), `RunBarComponent` (`src/app/nav/run-bar/`, which also handles its swipe to end, as the workout cards on Workouts handle theirs to delete), the header and its back link in `NavComponent` (`src/app/nav/`), and the logo in `LoopLogoComponent` (`src/app/shared/components/loop-logo/`). The welcome screen is `WelcomeComponent` (`src/app/welcome/`), opened by Today's `welcomeFirst` guard (`welcome-first.ts`), which then opens the setup, `SetupComponent` (`src/app/welcome/setup/`), with the sign-in shared with the You tab, `SignInComponent` (`src/app/account/sign-in/`). Today is `HomeComponent` (`src/app/home/`), showing `GetStartedComponent` (`src/app/home/get-started/`, its plan picked by `firstPlan()` in `src/app/models/built-in-plans.ts`, shown by `PlanCardComponent`) with no workouts, or `WeekStripComponent` (`src/app/home/week-strip/`), `DueTodayComponent` (`src/app/home/due-today/`) and `SuggestedWorkoutComponent` (`src/app/history/suggested-workout/`), shared with the workout summary; `/home` waits for the history to load, as `/history` does. The tabs' names, icons and the pages each holds are `TABS` in `tabs.ts`; the pages kept their addresses (`/home`, `/library`, `/history`, `/account`) when the tabs were renamed, as sign-in links come back to `/account` and Supabase only sends them to addresses it's been told. Routes in `src/app/app.routes.ts`. Moving off the old address is `AddressMove` (`src/app/nav/moved-banner/`), started in `main.ts`, with its card, `MovedBannerComponent`, at the top of `<main>`. A page whose code fails to load (renamed by a later deploy; Vercel answers for the old name with index.html) is loaded afresh by `reloadOnStaleCode` (`src/app/nav/stale-code.ts`), the router's navigation error handler in `main.ts`. How much of the page the bars cover is `--bars-height`, worked out in `styles.scss` with `:has()` from which bars are showing; pages use it to leave room. `viewport-fit=cover` in `index.html` makes `env(safe-area-inset-*)` work, for the gap above an iPhone's home bar. The Undo bar is `UndoBarComponent` (`src/app/nav/undo-bar/`), showing the offer that pages make through `UndoService` (`src/app/shared/undo/`). The morph is `WorkoutMorph` (`src/app/nav/workout-morph.ts`): the bar and ⌄ start a view transition around their own navigation (the router makes none, so other pages change at once), and `src/styles/_workout-morph.scss` pairs the bar with the workout page by view-transition-name, only while it runs. Help is `HelpComponent` (`src/app/help/`), its questions and answers `HELP_SECTIONS` in `help-topics.ts` (with `searchTopics()`), checked by `help-topics.spec.ts` (unique ids, short answers, links only to real pages); the ? links are `HelpLinkComponent` (`src/app/shared/components/help-link/`), drawn as an inline SVG so no word joins the text beside it.
- **Web, added to the Home Screen:** `manifest.webmanifest` makes it open on its own, without the browser's bars (`display: standalone`). `<meta name="theme-color">` is the page colour (`--page-bg`) of the theme in use: index.html sets it before the app loads, then `ThemeService` keeps it in step, so the browser's bars, and an installed app's, match the page.
- **Web, the icon:** the favicon is the ∞ and its light on the Start button's cyan: `public/favicon.svg` (the light moves round the loop in Firefox; other browsers show it still), `favicon.ico` for browsers without SVG icons, `apple-touch-icon.png` for an iPhone's home screen, and `icon-192.png` and `icon-512.png` for `manifest.webmanifest`, all drawn by `tools/build-favicon.mjs`. An iPhone app would use the same artwork, square, as its app icon.
- **Web, shared links:** `public/robots.txt` lets search engines in everywhere (without it, the app's page came back in its place, which they read as a broken file). `index.html` has a description for search results, and Open Graph and Twitter card tags, so a link to loopworkouts.com shows a title, a line and a picture when shared. The picture is `public/share.png` (1200 × 630): the name and tagline beside a phone at the start of Kettlebell full body, made by `tools/build-share-image.mjs` from a running `ng serve --port 4300`. Make it again when the workout screen's look changes. The words, and their longer versions for listings and posts, are in [marketing](../marketing.md).
- **Web, the tour:** `TourService` (`src/app/tour/tour.service.ts`) holds whether it's offered (`tour` in localStorage) and which tour and stop is showing, and `TOURS` (`tour-stops.ts`) each tour's stops, and where focus goes when it ends: the app's here, Create's (BLD-17, in [build-a-workout](build-a-workout.md)), the workout page's (RUN-13, in [run-a-workout](run-a-workout.md)) and the Plan page's (PLN-8, in [planning](planning.md)). Each stop is a page (or none, to stay on the page it's on), the parts it may light up (by `data-tour` attributes on them, the first found winning), and their words. The overlay, `TourComponent` (`src/app/tour/`), loads only once a tour starts (`@defer` in `app.component.html`); it opens each page with the router, waits for the part to show and scrolls it into view (adding room under `main` while it's on, if the page is too short to bring the part above the card), then lights it and its tab (`data-tour-tab` in the tab bar, or `data-tour-tabs` for the whole bar): a ring round each, and a dimmed SVG over the screen with a hole for each (a mask, its rects moving by CSS `x`, `y`, `width` and `height`). Today's offer is `TourOfferComponent` (`src/app/home/tour-offer/`). Progress's examples are `sampleHistory()` (`src/app/tour/sample-history.ts`), shown through `ShownHistory` (`src/app/history/shown-history.ts`), what Progress's parts read the history from: Progress provides one that swaps in the examples while `TourService.sampleHistory` is on and the history is empty; everywhere else it's the history itself.
- **iOS:** the tour would be the same stops, each switching the `TabView`'s selection, with a `.popoverTip` from TipKit or an overlay with a cut-out at the part's frame (via `anchorPreference`); post `UIAccessibility.post(notification: .screenChanged, argument:)` at each stop so VoiceOver reads it.
- **iOS:** a `TabView` with the same five tabs. The workout in progress is a bar above the tabs (`tabViewBottomAccessory`, as Music's mini player), opening the workout full screen with a zoom transition from the bar (`.navigationTransition(.zoom(sourceID:in:))`), and a leading `.swipeActions` (or a drag gesture, if the accessory doesn't take swipe actions) to end it; and a **Live Activity** on the lock screen and Dynamic Island, which also serves ALM-2 and CTL-1.
